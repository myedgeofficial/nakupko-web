Tuna patties

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tuna patties is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned tuna320 g
  • Potatoes500 g
  • Eggs1
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Lemons1
  • Tartar sauce1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  2. Mix with the drained tuna, egg, parsley and lemon zest.
  3. Shape into patties and coat in breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in oil for 3 minutes on each side.
  5. Serve with tartare sauce and salad.
