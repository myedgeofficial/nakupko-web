Bujta repa

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bujta repa is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sour turnip1 kg
  • Pork shoulder800 g
  • Millet150 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Sweet paprika1 tbsp
  • Bay leaves2 leaves
  • Lard2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the pork into pieces and simmer in 2 l of water with the bay leaves for 1 hour.
  2. Add the rinsed sour turnip and cook for 40 minutes.
  3. Add the rinsed millet and cook for 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Fry the garlic and paprika in lard and stir in.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
