Pumpkin soup

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pumpkin soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Butternut squash1 kg
  • Potatoes2
  • Onions1
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Pumpkin seed oil2 tbsp
  • Pumpkin seeds40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Peel and dice the squash and potato.
  2. Fry the onion in oil for 4 minutes, then add the squash, potato and 1 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 25 minutes until soft.
  4. Blend, stir in the cream and season.
  5. Serve with pumpkin seed oil and seeds.
