Homemade bread

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade bread is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour1 kg
  • Fresh yeast1
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Sunflower oil3 tbsp
  • Saltto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the yeast in lukewarm water with the sugar.
  2. Add the flour, salt and oil and knead for 10 minutes.
  3. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  4. Shape a loaf and let it rise for 30 more minutes.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes at 200 °C.
