Pancakes

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pancakes is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour250 g
  • Milk500 ml
  • Eggs3
  • Sugar2 tbsp
  • Sunflower oil3 tbsp
  • Jam1 jar
  • Hazelnut spread1 jar

Method in 5 steps

  1. Whisk the flour, milk, eggs, sugar and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter.
  2. Rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Oil a pan lightly and pour in a thin layer of batter.
  4. Cook about 1 minute per side.
  5. Spread with jam or chocolate spread and roll up.
