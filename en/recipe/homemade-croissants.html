Homemade croissants

  • ⏱ 4 h
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade croissants is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 4 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour500 g
  • Butter300 g
  • Milk250 ml
  • Dry yeast1 bag
  • Sugar60 g
  • Eggs1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Knead the flour, milk, yeast, sugar and salt into a dough and chill for 1 hour.
  2. Flatten the butter into a slab, wrap it in the dough and roll out. Fold in three.
  3. Repeat rolling and folding 3 times, chilling 30 minutes in between.
  4. Roll out, cut into triangles and roll them up. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
  5. Brush with egg and bake for 15–18 minutes at 200 °C.
