Creamy tomato soup

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Creamy tomato soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Peeled tomatoes800 g
  • Onions1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Basil1 bunch
  • White bread4 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion and garlic in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes and 500 ml of water and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Stir in the cream and season.
  5. Serve with basil and toast.
