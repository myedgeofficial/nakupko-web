Warm salad with chicken livers

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Warm salad with chicken livers is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken livers400 g
  • Mixed salad1 bag
  • Balsamic vinegar3 tbsp
  • Onions1
  • Butter20 g
  • Apples1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Clean and dry the livers.
  2. Fry the onion in butter, add the livers and cook for 4 minutes.
  3. Add the balsamic vinegar and season.
  4. Toss the leaves with the sliced apple.
  5. Spoon the warm livers and juices over the salad.
