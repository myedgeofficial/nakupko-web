Fried doughnut balls

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fried doughnut balls is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour300 g
  • Plain yoghurt2
  • Eggs2
  • Baking powder1 bag
  • Frying oil700 ml
  • Icing sugarto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the yoghurt, eggs, flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt into a thick batter.
  2. Heat the oil.
  3. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the oil.
  4. Fry for 2–3 minutes until golden, turning.
  5. Drain on paper and dust with icing sugar.
