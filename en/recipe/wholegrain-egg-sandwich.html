Wholegrain egg sandwich

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Wholegrain egg sandwich is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Seeded bread8 slices
  • Eggs4
  • Mayonnaise3 tbsp
  • Iceberg lettuce1
  • Tomatoes2

Method in 4 steps

  1. Hard-boil the eggs (10 minutes), peel and chop.
  2. Mix with mayonnaise and salt.
  3. Spread the egg mix on the bread.
  4. Add lettuce and tomato and top with another slice.
