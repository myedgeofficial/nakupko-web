Sour turnip stew with ribs

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sour turnip stew with ribs is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sour turnip800 g
  • Pork ribs600 g
  • Canned beans400 g
  • Potatoes3
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • White flour2 tbsp
  • Lard1 tbsp
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Simmer the ribs with bay leaves in 2 l of water for 40 minutes.
  2. Add the sour turnip and diced potatoes and cook for 40 minutes more.
  3. Add the drained beans.
  4. Brown the flour and garlic in lard and stir the roux into the stew.
  5. Boil for 5 minutes and season.
