Sweet and sour chicken

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sweet and sour chicken is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Canned pineapple400 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Ketchup4 tbsp
  • Vinegar3 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Cornflour2 tbsp
  • Rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Coat the diced chicken in half the cornflour and fry.
  3. Add the peppers and pineapple and fry for 3 minutes.
  4. Mix ketchup, vinegar, soy sauce, pineapple juice and the rest of the cornflour and pour in.
  5. Cook until thick and serve with rice.
