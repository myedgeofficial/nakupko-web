Baked apples

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Baked apples is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Apples4
  • Walnuts80 g
  • Honey4 tbsp
  • Raisins50 g
  • Cinnamon1 tsp
  • Butter30 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Core the apples.
  3. Mix the walnuts with honey, raisins and cinnamon and fill the apples.
  4. Top with a knob of butter and bake for 30 minutes.
