Pork chops with apples

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pork chops with apples is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork chops4
  • Apples2
  • Onions1
  • Butter30 g
  • Thyme1 bunch
  • Cream100 ml
  • New potatoes800 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water.
  2. Season the chops and fry them in butter for 4 minutes on each side.
  3. Set them aside and fry the onion and apple wedges in the pan.
  4. Add the thyme and cream, return the chops and warm for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve with the potatoes.
