Pasta with ham and peas

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pasta with ham and peas is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Farfalle400 g
  • Ham200 g
  • Frozen peas200 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Butter20 g
  • Parmesan50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta as on the packet.
  2. Fry the ham strips in butter.
  3. Add the peas and cream and simmer for 4 minutes.
  4. Toss in the pasta.
  5. Sprinkle with parmesan and pepper.
