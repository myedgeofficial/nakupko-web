Vanilla pudding with fruit

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vanilla pudding with fruit is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Milk1 l
  • Pudding powder2 bags
  • Sugar80 g
  • Raspberries200 g
  • Blueberries200 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Mix the pudding powder with sugar and a little cold milk.
  2. Boil the rest of the milk, stir in the pudding and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Pour into cups and let it cool.
  4. Top with raspberries and blueberries.
