Tortilla pizza

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tortilla pizza is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas4
  • Passata200 ml
  • Pizza cheese200 g
  • Ham150 g
  • Oreganoto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C.
  2. Lay the tortillas on a baking tray and spread with tomato sauce.
  3. Add ham and cheese and sprinkle with oregano.
  4. Bake for 6–8 minutes until the cheese melts.
