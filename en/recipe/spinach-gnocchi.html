Spinach gnocchi

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spinach gnocchi is a quick dish for busy days from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Gnocchi800 g
  • Spinach300 g
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook the gnocchi as on the packet.
  2. Fry the garlic in oil for 1 minute and add the spinach.
  3. Pour in the cream and simmer for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the drained gnocchi and parmesan and stir.
