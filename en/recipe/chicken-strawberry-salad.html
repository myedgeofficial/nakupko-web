Chicken strawberry salad

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken strawberry salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Mixed salad200 g
  • Strawberries250 g
  • Feta150 g
  • Walnuts50 g
  • Balsamic vinegarto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Season the chicken, fry for 5 minutes per side, then slice.
  2. Halve the strawberries.
  3. Put the salad leaves, strawberries, chicken and feta on plates.
  4. Sprinkle with walnuts and drizzle with balsamic.
