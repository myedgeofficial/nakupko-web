Hummus plate

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Hummus plate is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Hummus400 g
  • Pita bread4
  • Cucumbers1
  • Carrots2
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Olives100 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Warm the pitta bread in a pan or oven.
  2. Cut the cucumber and carrots into sticks and halve the tomatoes.
  3. Put the hummus in the middle of the plate.
  4. Arrange the veg, olives and bread around it.
