Buckwheat žganci

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 3 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Buckwheat žganci is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 3 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Buckwheat flour500 g
  • Pork cracklings150 g
  • Soured milk1 l

Method in 5 steps

  1. Bring 1 l of salted water to the boil.
  2. Tip the flour in as a heap, don't stir. Cook covered for 20 minutes.
  3. Poke a hole in the heap and cook for another 10 minutes.
  4. Pour off some water and break it up with a fork.
  5. Top with crackling and serve with soured milk.
