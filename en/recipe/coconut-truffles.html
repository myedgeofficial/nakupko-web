Coconut truffles

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Coconut truffles is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Desiccated coconut200 g
  • Mascarpone250 g
  • Icing sugar60 g
  • Ground hazelnuts50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the mascarpone, sugar and 150 g of coconut.
  2. Chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Shape into balls with a few nuts inside each.
  4. Roll in the remaining coconut.
  5. Keep in the fridge.
