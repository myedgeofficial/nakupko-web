Chicken fajitas

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken fajitas is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Bell peppers3
  • Onions2
  • Taco seasoning1 bag
  • Sour cream200 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cut the chicken, pepper and onion into strips.
  2. Fry the chicken for 5 minutes, add the veg and seasoning and fry for 5 more.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  4. Fill them and add sour cream.
