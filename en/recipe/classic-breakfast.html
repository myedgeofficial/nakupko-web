Classic breakfast

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Classic breakfast is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White bread8 slices
  • Butter50 g
  • Jamto taste
  • Eggs6
  • Plain yoghurt4
  • Ground coffeeto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Make the coffee.
  2. Soft-boil the eggs (6 minutes).
  3. Toast the bread and spread with butter and jam.
  4. Serve with yoghurt.
