Party snacks

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Party snacks is a quick dish for busy days from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Crisps2 bags
  • Smoki (peanut puffs)2 bags
  • Salted sticks2 bags
  • Peanuts200 g
  • Coca-Cola2 l
  • Beer (6-pack)1

Method in 3 steps

  1. Chill the drinks for at least 2 hours.
  2. Tip the snacks into bowls.
  3. Put everything on the table.
