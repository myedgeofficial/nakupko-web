Roasted veg with feta

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roasted veg with feta is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Courgettes2
  • Aubergines1
  • Bell peppers2
  • Red onions2
  • Feta200 g
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Cut the vegetables into chunks and spread on a tray.
  3. Drizzle with oil, season and roast for 30 minutes.
  4. Crumble the feta on top and roast for 5 more minutes.
