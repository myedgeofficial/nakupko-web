Bean soup

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bean soup is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned beans2 cans
  • Smoked ham300 g
  • Potatoes2
  • Carrots2
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dice the ham and simmer it in 1.5 l of water for 20 minutes.
  2. Add diced potato, carrot and bay leaves.
  3. Cook for 20 minutes.
  4. Add the beans and garlic and cook for 15 more minutes.
  5. Season.
