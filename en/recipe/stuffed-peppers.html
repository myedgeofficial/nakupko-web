Stuffed peppers

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Stuffed peppers is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bell peppers8
  • Minced meat500 g
  • Rice150 g
  • Onions1
  • Passata700 ml
  • Potatoes800 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Half-cook the rice (10 minutes) and let it cool.
  2. Mix the mince with the rice, chopped onion, salt and pepper.
  3. Cut the tops off the peppers, clean them and stuff them.
  4. Stand them in a pot, add the tomato sauce and water to almost cover.
  5. Simmer covered for 1 hour. Serve with potatoes.
