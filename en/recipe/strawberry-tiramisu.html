Strawberry tiramisu

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Strawberry tiramisu is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Ladyfingers300 g
  • Mascarpone500 g
  • Strawberries500 g
  • Icing sugar100 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Orange juice200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the mascarpone with icing sugar, whip the cream and fold it in.
  2. Slice the strawberries.
  3. Dip the biscuits quickly in orange juice and lay them in a dish.
  4. Alternate layers of cream, strawberries and biscuits.
  5. Chill for at least 3 hours.
