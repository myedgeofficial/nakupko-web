Toasted flour soup

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Toasted flour soup is a quick dish for busy days from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour60 g
  • Butter50 g
  • Eggs2
  • Caraway1 tsp
  • White bread4 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Melt the butter, add the flour and stir until brown.
  2. Add the caraway and pour in 1.5 l of water, stirring out any lumps.
  3. Simmer for 10 minutes and season.
  4. Stir in the beaten egg.
  5. Serve with toasted bread cubes.
