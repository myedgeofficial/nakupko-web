Spaghetti carbonara

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spaghetti carbonara is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Bacon200 g
  • Eggs4
  • Parmesan80 g
  • Black pepperto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
  2. Dice the bacon and crisp it in a pan.
  3. Whisk the eggs with grated parmesan and pepper.
  4. Drain the spaghetti (keep a little water) and add it to the bacon. Take the pan off the heat.
  5. Pour in the eggs, stir fast and add a splash of water until creamy.
