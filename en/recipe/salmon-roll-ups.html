Salmon roll-ups

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salmon roll-ups is a quick dish for busy days from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas4
  • Smoked salmon200 g
  • Cheese spread200 g
  • Rocket100 g

Method in 3 steps

  1. Spread the tortillas with cream cheese.
  2. Add the salmon and rocket.
  3. Roll tightly and slice into rounds.
