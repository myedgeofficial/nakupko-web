Stuffed peppers with rice and feta

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Stuffed peppers with rice and feta is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bell peppers8
  • Rice200 g
  • Feta200 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C.
  2. Half-cook the rice (10 minutes).
  3. Mix with the feta, chopped onion and parsley.
  4. Stuff the peppers, put them in a dish and pour over the tomatoes.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes.
