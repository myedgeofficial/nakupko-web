Tofu veggie pasta

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tofu veggie pasta is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tofu400 g
  • Penne400 g
  • Broccoli1
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook the pasta, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes.
  2. Cube the tofu and fry it in oil until golden.
  3. Add the garlic and soy sauce.
  4. Add the drained pasta and broccoli and toss.
