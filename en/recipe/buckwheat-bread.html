Buckwheat bread

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Buckwheat bread is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Buckwheat flour300 g
  • White flour300 g
  • Dry yeast1 bag
  • Walnuts80 g
  • Saltto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix both flours, yeast and salt, add about 400 ml of lukewarm water and knead.
  2. Work in the chopped walnuts.
  3. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
  4. Shape a loaf, put it in a tin and let it rise for 30 more minutes.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes at 200 °C.
