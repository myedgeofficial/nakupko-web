Potato goulash

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Potato goulash is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Frankfurters4
  • Onions2
  • Sweet paprika2 tbsp
  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Method in 4 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the paprika, tomato paste and diced potatoes and stir.
  3. Cover the potatoes with water, add bay leaves and simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Add the sliced hot dogs and cook for 5 more minutes. Season.
