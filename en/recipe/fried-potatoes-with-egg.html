Fried potatoes with egg

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fried potatoes with egg is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Onions2
  • Eggs4
  • Lard3 tbsp
  • Carawayto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel and slice.
  2. Fry the chopped onion in lard for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the potatoes and caraway and fry for 10 minutes, turning until crisp.
  4. Fry the eggs on top.
