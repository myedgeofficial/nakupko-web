Matevž with sauerkraut

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Matevž with sauerkraut is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned beans2 cans
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Pork cracklings100 g
  • Sauerkraut500 g
  • Blood sausages4

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes and mash with the drained beans.
  2. Top with crackling and season.
  3. Warm the sauerkraut.
  4. Bake the blood sausages for 20 minutes at 180 °C.
  5. Serve together.
