Turkey in mushroom sauce

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Turkey in mushroom sauce is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Turkey steaks600 g
  • Mushrooms300 g
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Onions1
  • Rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Season the turkey and fry for 3 minutes per side, then set aside.
  3. In the same pan fry the onion and mushrooms for 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the cream, return the turkey and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice.
