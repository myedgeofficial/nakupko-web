Cheesecake

  • ⏱ 5 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cheesecake is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 5 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Petit beurre biscuits250 g
  • Butter120 g
  • Mascarpone500 g
  • Cottage cheese (quark)500 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs3
  • Strawberries300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a tin.
  2. Mix the mascarpone, quark, sugar and eggs.
  3. Pour over the base and bake for 1 hour at 160 °C.
  4. Let it cool in the oven, then chill for at least 3 hours.
  5. Decorate with strawberries.
