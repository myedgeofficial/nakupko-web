Nuggets and wedges

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Nuggets and wedges is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Cornflakes150 g
  • Eggs2
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Ketchupto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Cut the potatoes into chips and roast with oil for 35 minutes.
  3. Crush the cornflakes and cut the chicken into pieces.
  4. Dip the pieces in egg, then cornflakes, and put on a tray.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes and serve with ketchup.
