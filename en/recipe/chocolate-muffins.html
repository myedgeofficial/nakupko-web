Chocolate muffins

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate muffins is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour250 g
  • Cocoa powder40 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs2
  • Milk250 ml
  • Sunflower oil100 ml
  • Chocolate chips100 g
  • Baking powder1 bag

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in one bowl and the wet in another.
  3. Combine them just until mixed. Add the chocolate chips.
  4. Divide the batter between muffin cases.
  5. Bake for 20 minutes.
