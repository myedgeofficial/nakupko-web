Potato salad

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Potato salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Red onions1
  • Vinegar3 tbsp
  • Pumpkin seed oil3 tbsp
  • Chives1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins (25 minutes), peel and slice while warm.
  2. Finely chop the onion.
  3. Mix the vinegar with a little warm water, salt and oil.
  4. Pour over the potatoes, add the onion and mix.
  5. Sprinkle with chives.
