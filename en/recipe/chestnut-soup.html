Chestnut soup

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chestnut soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chestnuts400 g
  • Onions1
  • Carrots1
  • Butter30 g
  • Cream150 ml
  • Stock cube1
  • Bacon80 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Score the chestnuts, roast at 200 °C for 20 minutes and peel.
  2. Fry the onion and carrot in butter.
  3. Add the chestnuts, 1 l of water and the stock cube and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the cream and blend.
  5. Serve with crispy bacon.
