Strawberry yoghurt cake

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Strawberry yoghurt cake is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Petit beurre biscuits200 g
  • Butter100 g
  • Plain yoghurt500 g
  • Cream250 ml
  • Sugar100 g
  • Gelatine2 bags
  • Strawberries500 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Crush the biscuits, mix with melted butter and press into a tin.
  2. Dissolve the gelatine as on the packet.
  3. Mix the yoghurt with the sugar and gelatine and fold in the whipped cream.
  4. Fold in half of the strawberries, sliced, and pour onto the base.
  5. Chill for 4 hours and decorate with the rest of the strawberries.
