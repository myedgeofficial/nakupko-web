Homemade cookies

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade cookies is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour300 g
  • Butter200 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs1
  • Chocolate chips150 g
  • Vanilla sugar1 bag

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Cream the soft butter with the sugar, then add the egg and vanilla sugar.
  3. Mix in the flour and chocolate chips.
  4. Put small heaps of dough on a tray.
  5. Bake for 12 minutes.
