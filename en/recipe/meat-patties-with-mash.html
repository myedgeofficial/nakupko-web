Meat patties with mash

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Meat patties with mash is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Minced meat700 g
  • Eggs1
  • White bread2 slices
  • Onions1
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Milk200 ml
  • Butter50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Soak the bread in milk and squeeze it.
  2. Mix the mince with the bread, egg, chopped onion and salt.
  3. Shape patties and fry in oil for 5 minutes per side.
  4. Boil the potatoes and mash with butter and milk.
  5. Serve together.
