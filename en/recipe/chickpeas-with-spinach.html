Chickpeas with spinach

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chickpeas with spinach is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chickpeas800 g
  • Spinach300 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Caraway1 tsp
  • Seeded bread4 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion, garlic and cumin in oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the drained chickpeas and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the spinach until wilted.
  5. Serve with bread.
