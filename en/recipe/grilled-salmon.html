Grilled salmon

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled salmon is a quick dish for busy days from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Salmon fillet800 g
  • Lemons1
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Dill1 bunch
  • Courgettes2

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the salmon and brush with oil.
  2. Grill skin side down for 6–8 minutes, then turn for 2 minutes.
  3. Slice and grill the courgettes.
  4. Squeeze over lemon.
  5. Sprinkle with dill.
