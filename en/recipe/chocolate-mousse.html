Chocolate mousse

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate mousse is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cooking chocolate200 g
  • Cream400 ml
  • Eggs3
  • Sugar40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Melt the chocolate over steam and let it cool a little.
  2. Stir the yolks into the chocolate.
  3. Whip the cream, and whisk the whites with the sugar until stiff.
  4. Gently fold the cream and whites into the chocolate.
  5. Spoon into glasses and chill for at least 3 hours.
