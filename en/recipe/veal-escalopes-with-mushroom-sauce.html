Veal escalopes with mushroom sauce

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Veal escalopes with mushroom sauce is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Veal escalopes600 g
  • Mushrooms300 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • White flour3 tbsp
  • Butter30 g
  • Rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Pound the escalopes, season and dust with flour.
  2. Fry in butter for 2 minutes on each side and set aside.
  3. Fry the sliced mushrooms in the pan for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cream, return the escalopes and simmer for 3 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice.
