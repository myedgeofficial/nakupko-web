Roast beef

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roast beef is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef sirloin1.2 kg
  • Mustard2 tbsp
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Horseradish1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the beef and sear it on all sides in oil.
  2. Brush with mustard and add rosemary and garlic.
  3. Roast at 200 °C for 35–40 minutes for medium.
  4. Rest covered for 15 minutes, then slice thinly.
  5. Serve with roast potatoes and fresh horseradish.
