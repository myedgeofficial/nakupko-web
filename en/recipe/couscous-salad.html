Couscous salad

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Couscous salad is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Couscous250 g
  • Bell peppers1
  • Cucumbers1
  • Cherry tomatoes200 g
  • Feta150 g
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Pour 250 ml of boiling salted water over the couscous and cover for 5 minutes.
  2. Dice the vegetables.
  3. Fluff the couscous and mix with the vegetables and parsley.
  4. Season with lemon juice, oil and salt.
  5. Sprinkle with crumbled feta.
