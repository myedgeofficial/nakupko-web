Fried courgettes with garlic dip

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fried courgettes with garlic dip is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Courgettes3
  • Eggs2
  • White flour100 g
  • Breadcrumbs150 g
  • Frying oil500 ml
  • Plain yoghurt200 g
  • Garlic2 cloves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Slice the courgettes into rounds and salt them.
  2. Coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Fry in oil until golden.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic and salt.
  5. Serve with the garlic dip.
