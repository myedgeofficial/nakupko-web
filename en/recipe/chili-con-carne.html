Chili con carne

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chili con carne is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Minced beef500 g
  • Canned kidney beans2 cans
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Canned sweetcorn1 can
  • Chilli powderto taste
  • Rice300 g

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the mince and brown it.
  3. Add the tomatoes, chilli, drained beans and sweetcorn.
  4. Simmer for 40 minutes, stirring now and then.
  5. Serve with rice.
