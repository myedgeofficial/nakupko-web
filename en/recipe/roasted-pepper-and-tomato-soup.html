Roasted pepper and tomato soup

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roasted pepper and tomato soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Red peppers4
  • Tomatoes6
  • Onions1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Stock cube1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the peppers, tomatoes, onion and garlic and spread them on a tray.
  2. Drizzle with oil and roast at 200 °C for 30 minutes.
  3. Tip the vegetables into a pot with 700 ml of water and the stock cube.
  4. Add the smoked paprika and blend.
  5. Bring to the boil and season.
