Prekmurje layer cake

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Challenging
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Prekmurje layer cake is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Filo pastry500 g
  • Cottage cheese (quark)1 kg
  • Poppy seeds250 g
  • Walnuts250 g
  • Apples1 kg
  • Raisins100 g
  • Sugar300 g
  • Sour cream400 g
  • Eggs4

Method in 5 steps

  1. Make four fillings: quark with egg and cream, poppy seeds with sugar, walnuts with sugar, grated apples with raisins.
  2. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  3. Lay a pastry sheet in a tin, then quark, poppy, walnut and apple in turn, covering each with a sheet.
  4. Repeat the layers and pour cream on top.
  5. Bake for 1½ hours.
