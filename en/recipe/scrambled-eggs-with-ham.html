Scrambled eggs with ham

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Scrambled eggs with ham is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs8
  • Ham150 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Chives1 bunch
  • White bread8 slices

Method in 4 steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Cut the ham into strips and warm it in butter for 1 minute.
  3. Add the eggs and stir slowly over medium heat until creamy.
  4. Sprinkle with chives and serve with bread.
