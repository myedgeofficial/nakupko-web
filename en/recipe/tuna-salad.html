Tuna salad

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tuna salad is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned tuna2 cans
  • Iceberg lettuce1
  • Canned sweetcorn1 can
  • Tomatoes3
  • Eggs4
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Hard-boil the eggs (10 minutes) and cool them.
  2. Chop the lettuce and cut the tomatoes into wedges.
  3. Drain the tuna and sweetcorn.
  4. Put everything in a bowl and add the quartered eggs.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and season.
