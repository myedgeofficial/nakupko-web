Butter chicken

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Butter chicken is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast700 g
  • Butter50 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Garam masala2 tsp
  • Ginger30 g
  • Basmati rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Fry the grated ginger and garam masala in butter for 1 minute.
  3. Add the diced chicken and brown it.
  4. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and serve with rice.
