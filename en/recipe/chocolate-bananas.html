Chocolate bananas

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate bananas is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bananas4
  • Cooking chocolate150 g
  • Hazelnut spread4 tbsp
  • Walnuts50 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Peel the bananas and halve them lengthways.
  2. Melt the chocolate over steam.
  3. Spread the bananas with chocolate spread and pour over the chocolate.
  4. Sprinkle with chopped walnuts.
