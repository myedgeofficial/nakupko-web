Paprika stew

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Paprika stew is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Bell peppers3
  • Onions2
  • Sweet paprika2 tbsp
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Egg noodles400 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Chop the onion and fry it in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add diced chicken and sliced peppers and fry for 5 minutes.
  3. Sprinkle with paprika, add a glass of water and simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the noodles.
  5. Stir in the sour cream at the end.
