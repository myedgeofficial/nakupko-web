Sausages with mash

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sausages with mash is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Grilling sausages4
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Milk200 ml
  • Butter50 g
  • Sauerkraut500 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water and mash with butter and milk.
  2. Fry the sausages in a little oil for 15 minutes, turning.
  3. Warm the sauerkraut.
  4. Serve together.
