Octopus with potatoes

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Octopus with potatoes is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Octopus1.5 kg
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Olive oil6 tbsp
  • Lemons1

Method in 4 steps

  1. Simmer the octopus in water for 1½ hours until tender.
  2. Cook the potatoes in the same water for the last 25 minutes.
  3. Cut up the octopus and halve the potatoes.
  4. Mix with oil, garlic, parsley and lemon and season.
