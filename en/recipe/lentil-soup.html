Lentil soup

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lentil soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lentils300 g
  • Carrots2
  • Onions1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Caraway1 tsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion, garlic and carrot in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the cumin, rinsed lentils, tomatoes and 1.5 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 30 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  4. Season. Blend part of the soup if you like.
