Stuffed roast chicken

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Stuffed roast chicken is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Whole chicken1
  • White bread300 g
  • Eggs2
  • Milk200 ml
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Butter60 g
  • Potatoes1 kg

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C.
  2. Cube the bread, pour over the milk and add the eggs, parsley and salt.
  3. Spoon the stuffing into the chicken.
  4. Rub the chicken with butter and salt and put the potatoes around it.
  5. Roast for 1½ hours.
