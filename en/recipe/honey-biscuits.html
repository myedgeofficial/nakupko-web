Honey biscuits

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Honey biscuits is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour500 g
  • Honey200 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs2
  • Butter50 g
  • Cinnamon1 tsp
  • Baking soda1 tsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Warm the honey, sugar and butter until melted and let cool.
  2. Add the eggs, flour, cinnamon and bicarb and knead.
  3. Roll out and cut shapes.
  4. Bake for 10 minutes at 180 °C.
