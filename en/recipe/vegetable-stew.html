Vegetable stew

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vegetable stew is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes4
  • Carrots3
  • Courgettes2
  • Green beans300 g
  • Tomatoes3
  • Onions1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Chop the onion and fry it in oil for 4 minutes.
  2. Add diced potato and carrot and 1.5 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add courgette, green beans and tomato and cook for 15 more minutes.
  5. Season.
