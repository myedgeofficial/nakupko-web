Broccoli cream soup

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Broccoli cream soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Broccoli2
  • Potatoes2
  • Onions1
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Stock cube1

Method in 4 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion in oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the broccoli, diced potato, stock cube and 1 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Blend and stir in the cream.
