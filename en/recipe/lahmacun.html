Lahmacun

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lahmacun is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Minced meat400 g
  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Onions1
  • Bell peppers1
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Lemons1
  • Sweet paprika1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Finely chop the onion, pepper and parsley.
  2. Mix with the raw mince, tomato paste, paprika and salt.
  3. Spread thinly over the tortillas.
  4. Bake at 230 °C for 8–10 minutes.
  5. Squeeze over lemon, add parsley and roll up.
