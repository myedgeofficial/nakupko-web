Vegetable curry with rice

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vegetable curry with rice is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cauliflower0.5
  • Sweet potatoes1
  • Chickpeas400 g
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Curry paste2 tbsp
  • Onions1
  • Basmati rice300 g
  • Spinach100 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion and curry paste in oil.
  2. Add the diced sweet potato and cauliflower, pour in the coconut milk and 200 ml of water.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Add the chickpeas and spinach and cook for 5 more minutes.
  5. Meanwhile cook the rice and serve together.
