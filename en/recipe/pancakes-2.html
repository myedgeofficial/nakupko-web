Pancakes

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pancakes is an everyday dish from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour250 g
  • Milk300 ml
  • Eggs2
  • Baking powder1 bag
  • Sugar2 tbsp
  • Maple syrupto taste
  • Blueberries250 g

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, milk and eggs into a thick batter.
  2. Oil a pan lightly.
  3. Pour in small rounds and cook until bubbles appear, then flip.
  4. Serve with blueberries and maple syrup.
