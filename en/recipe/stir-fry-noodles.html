Stir-fry noodles

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Stir-fry noodles is a quick dish for busy days from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chinese noodles400 g
  • Frozen stir-fry vegetables600 g
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Ginger20 g
  • Sesame oil2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the noodles as on the packet and drain.
  2. Fry garlic and grated ginger in a hot pan with oil for 1 minute.
  3. Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles and soy sauce and toss.
  5. Drizzle with sesame oil.
