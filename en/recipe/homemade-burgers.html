Homemade burgers

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade burgers is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Minced beef600 g
  • Burger buns4
  • Cheddar cheese4 slices
  • Iceberg lettuce1
  • Tomatoes2
  • Red onions1
  • BBQ sauceto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the mince, shape 4 patties and press them a bit flatter.
  2. Cook on a very hot pan or grill for 3–4 minutes per side.
  3. At the end lay cheese on top to melt.
  4. Halve and toast the buns.
  5. Build the burgers with lettuce, tomato, onion and BBQ sauce.
