Homemade noodles with mushrooms

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade noodles with mushrooms is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Egg noodles400 g
  • Mushrooms400 g
  • Onions1
  • Sour cream150 g
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Butter30 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the noodles in salted water.
  2. Fry the onion and mushrooms in butter for 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the sour cream and parsley.
  4. Add the drained noodles.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
