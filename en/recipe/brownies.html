Brownies

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Brownies is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cooking chocolate200 g
  • Butter150 g
  • Sugar200 g
  • Eggs3
  • White flour100 g
  • Walnuts80 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Melt the chocolate and butter.
  3. Stir in the sugar and eggs, then the flour and walnuts.
  4. Pour into a tin and bake for 25 minutes. The middle should stay fudgy.
  5. Cool and cut.
