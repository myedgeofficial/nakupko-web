Rosemary lamb

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Rosemary lamb is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lamb1.5 kg
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • White wine200 ml
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 170 °C.
  2. Rub the lamb with oil, salt, garlic and rosemary.
  3. Put it in a tin, add the wine and cover with foil.
  4. Roast for 2 hours, then uncover, add the potatoes and roast for 1 more hour.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes.
