Toasted sandwiches

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Toasted sandwiches is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Toast bread16 slices
  • Ham200 g
  • Sliced cheese200 g
  • Butter40 g
  • Ketchupto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Put ham and cheese on the unbuttered side and top with another slice, butter facing out.
  3. Toast in a pan or sandwich maker for 2–3 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Serve with ketchup.
