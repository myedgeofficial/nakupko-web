Chicken wraps

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken wraps is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Chicken breast500 g
  • Iceberg lettuce1
  • Tomatoes3
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Gouda cheese150 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the chicken into strips, season and fry for 6–8 minutes.
  2. Chop the lettuce and tomato.
  3. Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry pan.
  4. Spread sour cream on each tortilla, add chicken, veg and cheese.
  5. Roll up and serve.
