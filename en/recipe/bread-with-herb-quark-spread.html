Bread with herb quark spread

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bread with herb quark spread is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Brown bread8 slices
  • Cottage cheese (quark)250 g
  • Sour cream100 ml
  • Chives1 bunch
  • Spring onions2

Method in 3 steps

  1. Mix the quark with the sour cream and salt.
  2. Add finely chopped chives and spring onion.
  3. Spread on the bread.
