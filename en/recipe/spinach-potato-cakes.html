Spinach potato cakes

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spinach potato cakes is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Spinach200 g
  • Eggs2
  • Grated cheese100 g
  • White flour4 tbsp
  • Sunflower oil100 ml
  • Plain yoghurt200 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  2. Blanch the spinach, squeeze it and chop.
  3. Mix the potatoes, spinach, eggs, cheese and flour and salt.
  4. Shape into cakes and fry in oil until golden.
  5. Serve with garlic yoghurt.
