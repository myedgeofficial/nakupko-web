Caesar salad

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Caesar salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Romaine lettuce2
  • Parmesan60 g
  • White bread4 slices
  • Mayonnaise100 g
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the chicken, fry for 5 minutes per side, then slice.
  2. Cube the bread and fry it in a little oil.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with lemon juice and a little grated parmesan.
  4. Chop the lettuce and toss with the dressing.
  5. Add the chicken, croutons and parmesan.
