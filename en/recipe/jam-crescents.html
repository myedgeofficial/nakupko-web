Jam crescents

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Jam crescents is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour400 g
  • Butter250 g
  • Cottage cheese (quark)250 g
  • Jam1 jar
  • Icing sugar50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Knead the flour, butter and cottage cheese into a dough and chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll out and cut into triangles.
  3. Put jam on the wide end and roll into crescents.
  4. Bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes.
  5. Roll in icing sugar while still warm.
