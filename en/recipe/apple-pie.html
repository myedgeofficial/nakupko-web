Apple pie

  • ⏱ 1 h 20 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Apple pie is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour400 g
  • Butter200 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs2
  • Apples1.5 kg
  • Cinnamon2 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Rub the flour, butter, sugar and eggs into a shortcrust dough.
  2. Grate the apples and mix with cinnamon and a little sugar.
  3. Roll half the dough into a tin and add the apples.
  4. Cover with the other half of the dough.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes at 180 °C.
