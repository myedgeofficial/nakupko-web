Potato soup

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Potato soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes800 g
  • Carrots2
  • Onions1
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Marjoramto taste
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Dice the potatoes and carrots.
  2. Fry the onion in oil for 4 minutes, then add the veg, bay leaves and 1.5 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 25 minutes.
  4. Stir in sour cream and marjoram and season.
