Soup with liver dumplings

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Soup with liver dumplings is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef for soup500 g
  • Chicken livers200 g
  • Breadcrumbs100 g
  • Eggs1
  • Onions1
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Carrots2

Method in 5 steps

  1. Simmer the beef with the carrots in 2 l of water (or use ready-made stock).
  2. Mince or finely chop the livers.
  3. Mix with the breadcrumbs, egg, fried onion, parsley and salt.
  4. Shape into small dumplings.
  5. Simmer them in the strained soup for 10 minutes.
