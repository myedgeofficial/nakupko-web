Homemade sushi

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade sushi is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sushi rice400 g
  • Nori sheets8
  • Salmon fillet300 g
  • Avocado2
  • Cucumbers1
  • Rice vinegar4 tbsp
  • Soy sauceto taste
  • Wasabito taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Rinse and cook the rice as on the packet, then mix with rice vinegar and cool.
  2. Cut the salmon, avocado and cucumber into strips.
  3. Lay a nori sheet on a mat and spread a thin layer of rice on it.
  4. Add the filling and roll tightly.
  5. Slice with a wet knife and serve with soy sauce and wasabi.
