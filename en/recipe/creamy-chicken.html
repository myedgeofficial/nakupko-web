Creamy chicken

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Creamy chicken is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Cooking cream400 ml
  • Mushrooms300 g
  • Onions1
  • Rice300 g
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Cut the chicken into strips and brown it.
  3. Add chopped onion and sliced mushrooms and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the cream, season and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with rice.
