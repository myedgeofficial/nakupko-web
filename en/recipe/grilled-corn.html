Grilled corn

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 3 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled corn is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 3 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Corn on the cob4
  • Butter50 g
  • Sea saltto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 3 steps

  1. Boil the corn for 10 minutes.
  2. Grill it for 8–10 minutes, turning.
  3. Brush with butter and sprinkle with salt.
