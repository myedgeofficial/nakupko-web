Pork in natural gravy

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pork in natural gravy is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork escalopes600 g
  • Onions2
  • White flour2 tbsp
  • Butter30 g
  • Potatoes1 kg

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes.
  2. Pound the steaks, season and dust with flour.
  3. Fry in butter for 3 minutes per side and set aside.
  4. Fry the onion in the same pan, add a glass of water and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Return the steaks to the sauce and serve with potatoes.
