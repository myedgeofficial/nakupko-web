Vegetable risotto

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vegetable risotto is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Risotto rice320 g
  • Courgettes1
  • Carrots1
  • Mushrooms250 g
  • Stock cube1
  • Parmesan60 g
  • Butter40 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cut the vegetables into small cubes and fry in butter for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the rice and toast for 1 minute.
  3. Add hot stock bit by bit, stirring, for 18 minutes.
  4. Stir in butter and parmesan.
