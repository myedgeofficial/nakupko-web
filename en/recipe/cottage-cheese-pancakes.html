Cottage cheese pancakes

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cottage cheese pancakes is an everyday dish from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cottage cheese (quark)400 g
  • Eggs2
  • White flour80 g
  • Sugar2 tbsp
  • Vanilla sugar1 bag
  • Plain yoghurt200 g
  • Blueberries125 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the cottage cheese, eggs, flour, sugar and vanilla sugar.
  2. Drop spoonfuls into a pan with a little oil.
  3. Fry for 2–3 minutes on each side.
  4. Serve with yoghurt.
  5. Top with blueberries.
