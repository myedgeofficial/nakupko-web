Chicken teriyaki

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken teriyaki is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Teriyaki sauce150 ml
  • Jasmine rice300 g
  • Broccoli1
  • Sesame seeds2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Dice the chicken and fry for 6 minutes.
  3. Add the teriyaki sauce and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Boil the broccoli for 4 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice and sprinkle with sesame.
