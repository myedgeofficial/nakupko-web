Bacon-wrapped pork tenderloin

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bacon-wrapped pork tenderloin is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork tenderloin800 g
  • Bacon150 g
  • New potatoes800 g
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Mustard2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Season the tenderloin, brush with mustard and wrap in bacon.
  3. Halve the potatoes and put them in a tin with rosemary.
  4. Put the meat in the middle and roast for 35–40 minutes.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and slice.
