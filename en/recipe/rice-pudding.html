Rice pudding

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Rice pudding is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pudding rice200 g
  • Milk1 l
  • Sugar60 g
  • Cinnamonto taste
  • Vanilla sugar1 bag

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Put the rice, milk, sugar and vanilla sugar in a pot.
  2. Bring to the boil and simmer very gently for 25–30 minutes, stirring often.
  3. When the rice is soft and creamy, take it off the heat.
  4. Sprinkle with cinnamon.
