Mushroom risotto

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Mushroom risotto is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Risotto rice320 g
  • Mushrooms400 g
  • Onions1
  • White wine150 ml
  • Parmesan60 g
  • Butter40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion in butter for 3 minutes, add sliced mushrooms and fry for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the rice and toast for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the wine and let it bubble away.
  4. Add hot water or stock bit by bit, stirring, for 18 minutes.
  5. Stir in butter and parmesan.
