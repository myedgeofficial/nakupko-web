Apple strudel

  • ⏱ 1 h 20 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Apple strudel is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Filo pastry500 g
  • Apples1.5 kg
  • Sugar150 g
  • Cinnamon2 tsp
  • Raisins80 g
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Butter100 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Peel and grate the apples and mix with sugar, cinnamon and raisins.
  3. Toast the breadcrumbs in butter.
  4. Brush the pastry sheets with melted butter, sprinkle with crumbs and add the apples.
  5. Roll up, brush with butter and bake for 45 minutes.
