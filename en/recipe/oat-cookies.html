Oat cookies

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Oat cookies is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Rolled oats200 g
  • White flour150 g
  • Butter120 g
  • Brown sugar120 g
  • Eggs1
  • Raisins80 g
  • Baking powder1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar and add the egg.
  3. Stir in the oats, flour, baking powder and raisins.
  4. Put small heaps on a tray and flatten them.
  5. Bake for 12–14 minutes.
