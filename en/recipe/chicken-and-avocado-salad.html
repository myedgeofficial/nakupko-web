Chicken and avocado salad

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken and avocado salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Avocado2
  • Lettuce1
  • Canned sweetcorn200 g
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Limes1
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the chicken and fry for 6 minutes on each side, then slice.
  2. Tear the lettuce, halve the tomatoes and slice the avocado.
  3. Add the drained corn.
  4. Whisk the oil, lime juice and salt.
  5. Top the salad with chicken and drizzle.
