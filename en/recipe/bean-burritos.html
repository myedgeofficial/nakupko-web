Bean burritos

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bean burritos is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Canned kidney beans2 cans
  • Rice200 g
  • Grated cheese150 g
  • Salsa dip300 g
  • Sour cream200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Drain the beans and warm them with a little salsa.
  3. Warm the tortillas.
  4. Fill with rice, beans, cheese and sour cream.
  5. Roll up and serve with salsa.
