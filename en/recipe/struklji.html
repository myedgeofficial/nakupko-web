Štruklji

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Štruklji is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Frozen štruklji1 kg
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Butter60 g
  • Sour cream200 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook the štruklji in salted water as on the packet (about 20 minutes).
  2. Toast the breadcrumbs in butter until golden.
  3. Sprinkle the drained štruklji with the crumbs.
  4. Serve with sour cream.
