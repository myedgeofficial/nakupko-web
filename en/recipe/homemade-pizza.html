Homemade pizza

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade pizza is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pizza dough2
  • Passata300 g
  • Mozzarella250 g
  • Ham200 g
  • Mushrooms200 g
  • Oreganoto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven as high as it goes (250 °C).
  2. Roll the dough out onto a baking tray.
  3. Spread with tomato purée and sprinkle with oregano.
  4. Add mozzarella, ham and mushrooms.
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are golden.
