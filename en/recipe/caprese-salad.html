Caprese salad

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Caprese salad is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tomatoes4
  • Mozzarella250 g
  • Basil1 bunch
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Balsamic vinegarto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 3 steps

  1. Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella.
  2. Lay them alternately on a plate and add basil leaves.
  3. Drizzle with oil and balsamic and season.
