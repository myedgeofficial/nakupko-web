Fruit yoghurt parfait

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fruit yoghurt parfait is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Greek yoghurt500 g
  • Granola150 g
  • Strawberries250 g
  • Blueberries125 g
  • Honey3 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Spoon the yoghurt into glasses.
  2. Add a layer of granola.
  3. Top with strawberries and blueberries.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
