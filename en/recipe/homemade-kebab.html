Homemade kebab

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Homemade kebab is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Pita bread4
  • Plain yoghurt200 g
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Tomatoes2
  • Iceberg lettuce0.5
  • Red onions1
  • Chicken seasoning1 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the chicken into thin strips and season.
  2. Fry in a hot pan for 8 minutes.
  3. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic and salt.
  4. Slice the vegetables.
  5. Warm the pitta and fill with meat, vegetables and sauce.
