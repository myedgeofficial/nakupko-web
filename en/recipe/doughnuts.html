Doughnuts

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Doughnuts is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour500 g
  • Fresh yeast1
  • Milk250 ml
  • Butter60 g
  • Eggs3
  • Sugar60 g
  • Jam1 jar
  • Frying oil1 l
  • Icing sugar50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the yeast in warm milk, add the flour, butter, eggs and sugar and knead for 10 minutes.
  2. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
  3. Roll out a finger thick and cut circles. Rise for 30 more minutes.
  4. Fry in oil at 170 °C for 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Fill with jam and dust with icing sugar.
