Chicken skewers

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken skewers is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast800 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Red onions2
  • Courgettes1
  • BBQ seasoningto taste
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cut the chicken, pepper, onion and courgette into chunks.
  2. Toss with oil and seasoning and thread onto skewers.
  3. Grill or pan-fry for 12–15 minutes, turning.
  4. Serve.
