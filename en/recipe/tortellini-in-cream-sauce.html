Tortellini in cream sauce

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tortellini in cream sauce is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Fresh tortellini750 g
  • Cooking cream400 ml
  • Ham150 g
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook the tortellini as on the packet.
  2. Chop the ham and fry it briefly.
  3. Pour in the cream and simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the tortellini and parmesan and stir.
