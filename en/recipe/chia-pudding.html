Chia pudding

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chia pudding is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chia seeds80 g
  • Milk400 ml
  • Honey2 tbsp
  • Raspberries125 g
  • Bananas1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the chia seeds, milk and honey.
  2. Stir again after 5 minutes.
  3. Refrigerate overnight.
  4. Divide into glasses in the morning.
  5. Top with fruit.
