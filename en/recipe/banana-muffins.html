Banana muffins

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Banana muffins is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bananas3
  • White flour250 g
  • Brown sugar120 g
  • Eggs2
  • Butter80 g
  • Baking powder1 bag
  • Cinnamon1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Mash the bananas and add the melted butter, eggs and sugar.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder and cinnamon.
  4. Divide into muffin cases.
  5. Bake for 20–22 minutes.
