Chocolate cake

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate cake is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour250 g
  • Cocoa powder60 g
  • Sugar250 g
  • Eggs3
  • Butter150 g
  • Cooking chocolate200 g
  • Cream250 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar, then add the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and cocoa and bake in a tin for 35 minutes.
  4. Melt the chocolate in hot cream.
  5. Pour the glaze over the cooled cake.
