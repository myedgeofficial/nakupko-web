Parisian schnitzel

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Parisian schnitzel is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Turkey steaks600 g
  • Eggs3
  • White flour100 g
  • Sunflower oil200 ml
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Lemons1

Method in 4 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes.
  2. Season the escalopes and coat in flour, then beaten egg.
  3. Fry in oil for 3 minutes per side.
  4. Serve with potatoes and lemon.
