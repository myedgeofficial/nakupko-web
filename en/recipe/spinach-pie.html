Spinach pie

  • ⏱ 1 h 10 min
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spinach pie is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 1 h 10 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Filo pastry500 g
  • Spinach600 g
  • Feta300 g
  • Eggs3
  • Butter80 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Wilt the spinach briefly and squeeze it dry.
  3. Mix with the feta and eggs.
  4. Brush the pastry sheets with melted butter, add the filling and roll up.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes.
