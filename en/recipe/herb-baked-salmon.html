Herb-baked salmon

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Herb-baked salmon is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Salmon fillet600 g
  • Lemons1
  • Dill1 bunch
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Green beans400 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Put the salmon in a dish and drizzle with oil and lemon juice.
  2. Sprinkle with chopped dill, garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile boil the green beans in salted water for 6 minutes.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.
