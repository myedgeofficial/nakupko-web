Pan meat pie

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pan meat pie is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Filo pastry1 pack
  • Minced meat500 g
  • Onions2
  • Plain yoghurt200 g
  • Eggs1
  • Sunflower oil80 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onions and mince and season.
  2. Brush the pastry sheets with oil.
  3. Spread meat on each sheet and roll into a sausage.
  4. Coil the rolls in a greased tin.
  5. Brush with yoghurt and egg and bake at 200 °C for 35 minutes.
