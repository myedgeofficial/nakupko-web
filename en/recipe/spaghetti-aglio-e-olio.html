Spaghetti aglio e olio

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spaghetti aglio e olio is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Olive oil6 tbsp
  • Chilli peppers1
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Bring a big pot of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti as on the packet.
  2. Meanwhile, gently fry sliced garlic and chilli in olive oil for 2 minutes. Don't let the garlic brown.
  3. Drain the spaghetti and keep half a cup of the cooking water.
  4. Tip the spaghetti into the pan, add a splash of cooking water and toss.
  5. Sprinkle with chopped parsley and parmesan.
