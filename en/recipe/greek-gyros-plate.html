Greek gyros plate

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Greek gyros plate is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork neck600 g
  • Frozen fries600 g
  • Plain yoghurt200 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Tomatoes2
  • Red onions1
  • Oregano1 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the meat into thin strips and season with oregano, paprika and salt.
  2. Fry in a hot pan until crispy.
  3. Bake the chips in the oven.
  4. For tzatziki mix the yoghurt, grated cucumber and garlic.
  5. Serve the meat with chips, vegetables and tzatziki.
