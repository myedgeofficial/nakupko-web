Banana bread

  • ⏱ 1 h 10 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Banana bread is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bananas3
  • White flour250 g
  • Eggs2
  • Brown sugar120 g
  • Butter100 g
  • Baking soda1 tsp
  • Walnuts80 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C.
  2. Mash the bananas with a fork.
  3. Add the melted butter, eggs and sugar and mix.
  4. Stir in the flour, bicarb and chopped walnuts.
  5. Pour into a loaf tin and bake for 55–60 minutes.
