Chocolate chip cookies

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate chip cookies is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour300 g
  • Butter170 g
  • Brown sugar150 g
  • Sugar50 g
  • Eggs1
  • Chocolate chips200 g
  • Baking soda1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Cream the soft butter with both sugars and add the egg.
  3. Mix in the flour, bicarb and chocolate chips.
  4. Put heaps on a tray with space between them.
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes. They firm up as they cool.
