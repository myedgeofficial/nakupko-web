Thai coconut soup

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Thai coconut soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Curry paste2 tbsp
  • Mushrooms200 g
  • Ginger1
  • Limes2
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Coriander (fresh)1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the curry paste and grated ginger in a little oil.
  2. Pour in the coconut milk and 700 ml of water and bring to the boil.
  3. Add the chicken strips and mushrooms and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Season with soy sauce and lime juice.
  5. Serve with coriander.
