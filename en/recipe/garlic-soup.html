Garlic soup

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Garlic soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Garlic10 cloves
  • Potatoes3
  • Butter30 g
  • White flour1 tbsp
  • White bread4 slices
  • Emmental cheese80 g
  • Stock cube1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Gently fry the sliced garlic in butter without browning.
  2. Dust with flour, add 1 l of water with the stock cube and the diced potatoes.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes and blend.
  4. Cube the bread and toast it.
  5. Serve with croutons and cheese.
