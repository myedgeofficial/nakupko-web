Steak with vegetables

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Steak with vegetables is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef steak800 g
  • New potatoes800 g
  • Green beans400 g
  • Butter40 g
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Garlic3 cloves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes and the green beans for 6.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan for 2–3 minutes per side.
  3. Add butter, garlic and rosemary and baste for 1 minute.
  4. Rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with the vegetables.
