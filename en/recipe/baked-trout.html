Baked trout

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Baked trout is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Trout4
  • Lemons2
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • New potatoes800 g
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Season the trout and fill with garlic, parsley and lemon slices.
  3. Halve the potatoes, toss with oil and roast for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the trout and roast for 20 more minutes.
