Enchiladas

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Enchiladas is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Minced beef500 g
  • Canned kidney beans400 g
  • Salsa dip400 g
  • Cheddar cheese150 g
  • Onions1
  • Taco seasoning1 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion and mince with the Mexican seasoning.
  2. Add the beans and half the salsa.
  3. Fill and roll the tortillas and lay them in a dish.
  4. Pour over the remaining salsa and sprinkle with cheese.
  5. Bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes.
