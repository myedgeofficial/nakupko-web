Honey roast pork ribs

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Honey roast pork ribs is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork ribs1.5 kg
  • Honey3 tbsp
  • Soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Potatoes1 kg

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the ribs with salt and paprika and cover with foil.
  2. Roast at 160 °C for 1½ hours.
  3. Mix the honey, soy sauce and crushed garlic.
  4. Uncover, brush with the glaze, add potato wedges and roast at 200 °C for 30 minutes.
  5. Glaze once more halfway.
