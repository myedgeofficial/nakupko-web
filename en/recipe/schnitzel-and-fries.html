Schnitzel and fries

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Schnitzel and fries is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork escalopes600 g
  • Eggs2
  • White flour100 g
  • Breadcrumbs150 g
  • Frying oil500 ml
  • Frozen fries800 g
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Pound the escalopes thin and season them.
  2. Set out three plates: flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Coat each escalope in flour, egg, then crumbs.
  4. Fry in hot oil for 2–3 minutes per side.
  5. Cook the chips as on the packet and serve with lemon.
