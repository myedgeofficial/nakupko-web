Chicken noodle soup

  • ⏱ 1 h 15 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken noodle soup is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 1 h 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken thighs600 g
  • Carrots3
  • Celeriac1
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Soup pasta stars150 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Put the chicken in a pot with 2.5 l of cold water and bring to the boil.
  2. Skim off the foam, add the cleaned vegetables and salt.
  3. Simmer gently for 1 hour.
  4. Lift out the meat and vegetables and strain the soup.
  5. Cook the noodles in the soup, add the chopped meat back and the parsley.
