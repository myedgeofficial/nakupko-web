Roast chicken

  • ⏱ 1 h 40 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roast chicken is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Whole chicken1
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Garlic6 cloves
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Rub the chicken with oil, salt and lemon. Put garlic and rosemary inside.
  3. Cut the potatoes into wedges and arrange around the chicken.
  4. Roast for about 1 hour 20 minutes until the skin is golden.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, then carve.
