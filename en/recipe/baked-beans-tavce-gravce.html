Baked beans (tavče gravče)

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Baked beans (tavče gravče) is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Dried beans500 g
  • Onions2
  • Sweet paprika1 tbsp
  • Chilli peppers1
  • Sunflower oil4 tbsp
  • Mint1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Soak the beans overnight and boil for 1 hour.
  2. Fry the onions and paprika in oil.
  3. Mix with the beans and some cooking water.
  4. Pour into an earthenware dish and bake at 200 °C for 40 minutes.
  5. Sprinkle with mint.
