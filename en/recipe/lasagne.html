Lasagne

  • ⏱ 1 h 45 min
  • Challenging
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lasagne is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 1 h 45 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lasagne sheets250 g
  • Minced meat500 g
  • Passata700 ml
  • Milk500 ml
  • Butter50 g
  • White flour50 g
  • Mozzarella250 g
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Make the meat sauce: brown the mince, add the tomato sauce and simmer for 20 minutes.
  2. White sauce: melt the butter, stir in the flour, then the milk while stirring. Cook until thick.
  3. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  4. Layer sheets, meat sauce and white sauce in a dish. Top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes and let it stand for 10 minutes.
