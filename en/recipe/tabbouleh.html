Tabbouleh

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tabbouleh is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bulgur200 g
  • Parsley2 bunches
  • Tomatoes3
  • Cucumbers1
  • Lemons2
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Pour boiling water over the bulgur, cover for 15 minutes, then cool.
  2. Finely chop the parsley and dice the tomato and cucumber.
  3. Mix everything.
  4. Add lemon juice and oil and season.
