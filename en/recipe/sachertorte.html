Sachertorte

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Challenging
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sachertorte is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cooking chocolate300 g
  • Butter150 g
  • Sugar150 g
  • Eggs6
  • White flour130 g
  • Jam1 jar
  • Cream200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 170 °C.
  2. Melt half the chocolate, cream the butter and sugar, then add the yolks and chocolate.
  3. Whisk the whites stiff and fold them in with the flour. Bake for 50 minutes.
  4. Cut the cooled cake in half, spread with jam and put back together.
  5. Melt the rest of the chocolate in hot cream and pour over the cake.
