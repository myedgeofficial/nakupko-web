Pumpkin pie

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pumpkin pie is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Butternut squash800 g
  • White flour250 g
  • Butter125 g
  • Eggs3
  • Brown sugar150 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Cinnamon2 tsp
  • Ginger1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut up the squash, bake or boil until soft, then mash.
  2. Knead the flour and butter with a little water into a dough and line a tin.
  3. Mix the squash with the eggs, sugar, cream, cinnamon and grated ginger.
  4. Pour into the pastry case.
  5. Bake for 50 minutes at 180 °C and let it cool.
