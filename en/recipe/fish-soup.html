Fish soup

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fish soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Hake fillet500 g
  • Potatoes2
  • Onions1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • White wine150 ml
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • White bread4 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion and garlic in olive oil.
  2. Add the wine, tomatoes, diced potatoes and 1 l of water. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the fish in pieces and cook for 8 more minutes.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and parsley.
  5. Serve with toasted bread.
