Meat-free barley stew

  • ⏱ 1 h 10 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Meat-free barley stew is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 1 h 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pearl barley200 g
  • Canned beans400 g
  • Carrots2
  • Potatoes2
  • Onions1
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Bay leaves2 leaves
  • Stock cube1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Simmer the barley in 2 l of water with the bay leaves for 40 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped vegetables and the stock cube.
  3. Cook for another 25 minutes.
  4. Add the beans and smoked paprika.
  5. Season and serve.
