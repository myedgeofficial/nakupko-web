Spaghetti bolognese

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spaghetti bolognese is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Minced meat500 g
  • Onions1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Carrots1
  • Passata700 ml
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Finely chop the onion, garlic and carrot.
  2. Fry them in oil for 5 minutes, then add the mince and brown it.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, season and simmer for 30 minutes.
  4. When the sauce is nearly ready, cook the spaghetti.
  5. Serve with parmesan.
