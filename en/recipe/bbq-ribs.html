BBQ ribs

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

BBQ ribs is a weekend or holiday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork ribs1.5 kg
  • BBQ sauce300 ml
  • Honey2 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 150 °C.
  2. Rub the ribs with salt, smoked paprika and garlic.
  3. Wrap them in foil and bake for 2½ hours.
  4. Mix the BBQ sauce with the honey.
  5. Unwrap, brush with sauce and bake for another 20 minutes at 220 °C.
