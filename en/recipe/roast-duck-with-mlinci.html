Roast duck with mlinci

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Challenging
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roast duck with mlinci is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Duck1
  • Mlinci (flatbread)400 g
  • Red cabbage1
  • Apples2
  • Marjoramto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 170 °C.
  2. Rub the duck with salt and marjoram and put an apple inside.
  3. Roast for 2½ hours, basting with the fat.
  4. Shred the cabbage and braise it with apple for 30 minutes.
  5. Pour boiling water over the mlinci, drain and drizzle with duck fat.
