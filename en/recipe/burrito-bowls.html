Burrito bowls

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Burrito bowls is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Rice300 g
  • Canned kidney beans1 can
  • Canned sweetcorn1 can
  • Minced beef400 g
  • Avocado2
  • Salsa dip200 g
  • Sour cream200 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Brown and season the mince.
  3. Drain and warm the beans and sweetcorn.
  4. Slice the avocado.
  5. Fill bowls with rice, meat, beans, corn, avocado, salsa and sour cream.
