Halloumi with couscous

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Halloumi with couscous is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Halloumi250 g
  • Couscous250 g
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Mint1 bunch
  • Lemons1
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Pour 250 ml of boiling salted water over the couscous and cover for 5 minutes.
  2. Chop the tomatoes and cucumber and the mint.
  3. Fluff the couscous and mix with the vegetables, oil and lemon juice.
  4. Slice the halloumi and fry in a dry pan for 2 minutes on each side.
  5. Serve the cheese on the couscous.
