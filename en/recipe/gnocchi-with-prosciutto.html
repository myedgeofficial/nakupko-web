Gnocchi with prosciutto

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Gnocchi with prosciutto is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Fresh gnocchi800 g
  • Prosciutto100 g
  • Butter60 g
  • Parmesan50 g
  • Rocket50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the gnocchi until they float.
  2. Fry the prosciutto strips in butter.
  3. Add the drained gnocchi and fry for 3 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with parmesan.
  5. Serve with rocket.
