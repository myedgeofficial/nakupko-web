French onion soup

  • ⏱ 1 h 10 min
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

French onion soup is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 1 h 10 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Onions1 kg
  • Butter50 g
  • White flour1 tbsp
  • White wine150 ml
  • Beef stock cube2
  • White bread4 slices
  • Emmental cheese150 g
  • Thyme1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Slice the onions thinly and slowly caramelise them in butter for 40 minutes.
  2. Dust with flour, add the wine, 1.2 l of stock and the thyme.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Ladle into ovenproof bowls, top with bread and grated cheese.
  5. Grill in the oven until the cheese is golden.
