Prosciutto and cheese toastie

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Prosciutto and cheese toastie is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Toast bread8 slices
  • Prosciutto100 g
  • Gouda cheese8 slices
  • Rocket50 g
  • Butter20 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Butter the bread on one side.
  2. Put cheese and prosciutto on the unbuttered side and cover with another slice.
  3. Fry in a pan for 3 minutes on each side.
  4. Open and add the rocket.
  5. Cut and serve.
