Salmon cream pasta

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salmon cream pasta is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tagliatelle400 g
  • Smoked salmon200 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Dill1 bunch
  • Lemons1
  • Butter20 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta.
  2. Warm the cream with lemon zest in butter.
  3. Add the salmon strips and dill.
  4. Toss in the pasta.
  5. Season with pepper and serve.
