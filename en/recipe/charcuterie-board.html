Charcuterie board

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Charcuterie board is a quick dish for busy days from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Prosciutto150 g
  • Salami150 g
  • Tolminc cheese200 g
  • Gouda cheese200 g
  • Olives150 g
  • Grapes300 g
  • Crackers1 pack

Method in 3 steps

  1. Cut the cheese into cubes or slices.
  2. Arrange the prosciutto and salami on a board.
  3. Add the cheese, olives, grapes and crackers.
