Lemon cream pots

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lemon cream pots is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Mascarpone250 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Lemons2
  • Icing sugar80 g
  • Petit beurre biscuits100 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the mascarpone with icing sugar, lemon zest and juice.
  2. Whip the cream and gently fold it in.
  3. Crush the biscuits.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream in glasses.
  5. Chill for 1 hour.
