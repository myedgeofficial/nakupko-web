Niçoise salad

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Niçoise salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned tuna320 g
  • New potatoes500 g
  • Green beans250 g
  • Eggs4
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Olives80 g
  • Mixed salad1 bag
  • Mustard1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes, adding the green beans for the last 5 minutes.
  2. Hard-boil the eggs and halve them.
  3. Whisk oil, vinegar, mustard and salt for the dressing.
  4. Arrange the leaves, potatoes, beans, tomatoes, olives and tuna on plates.
  5. Add the eggs and drizzle with the dressing.
