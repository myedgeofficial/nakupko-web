Chicken skewers with yoghurt dip

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken skewers with yoghurt dip is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast800 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Red onions2
  • Greek yoghurt300 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Garlic2 cloves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the chicken, pepper and onion into chunks and thread onto skewers.
  2. Season and drizzle with oil.
  3. Grill or pan-fry for 12–15 minutes, turning.
  4. Mix the yoghurt with grated cucumber and crushed garlic.
  5. Serve with the yoghurt sauce.
