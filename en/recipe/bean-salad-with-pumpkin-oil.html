Bean salad with pumpkin oil

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bean salad with pumpkin oil is a quick dish for busy days from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Canned beans2 cans
  • Red onions1
  • Pumpkin seed oil4 tbsp
  • Vinegar2 tbsp

Method in 3 steps

  1. Drain and rinse the beans.
  2. Finely chop the onion.
  3. Mix the beans, onion, pumpkin seed oil, vinegar and salt.
