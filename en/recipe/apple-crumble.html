Apple crumble

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Apple crumble is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Apples1 kg
  • White flour150 g
  • Butter120 g
  • Brown sugar120 g
  • Rolled oats80 g
  • Cinnamon1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Peel and slice the apples, toss with cinnamon and put in a dish.
  3. Rub the flour, butter, sugar and oats together into crumbs.
  4. Scatter over the apples.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes until golden.
