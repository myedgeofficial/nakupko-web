Strawberry dumplings

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Strawberry dumplings is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cottage cheese (quark)500 g
  • White flour200 g
  • Eggs2
  • Strawberries16
  • Breadcrumbs150 g
  • Butter80 g
  • Icing sugar30 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Knead the quark, eggs and flour into a soft dough.
  2. Divide it and wrap each piece around a strawberry.
  3. Simmer in salted water for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Roll in crumbs toasted in butter.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.
