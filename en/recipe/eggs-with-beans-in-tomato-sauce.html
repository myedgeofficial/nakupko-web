Eggs with beans in tomato sauce

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Eggs with beans in tomato sauce is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs4
  • Canned beans400 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Seeded bread4 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion and paprika in oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer for 8 minutes.
  3. Make hollows and crack in the eggs.
  4. Cover and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with bread.
