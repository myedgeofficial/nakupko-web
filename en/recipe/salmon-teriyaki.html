Salmon teriyaki

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salmon teriyaki is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Salmon fillet600 g
  • Teriyaki sauce150 ml
  • Jasmine rice300 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Sesame seeds2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Fry the salmon for 4 minutes per side.
  3. Add the teriyaki sauce and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Slice the cucumber.
  5. Serve with rice and cucumber and sprinkle with sesame.
