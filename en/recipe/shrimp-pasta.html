Shrimp pasta

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Shrimp pasta is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tagliatelle400 g
  • Prawns400 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water.
  2. Fry the garlic in olive oil for 1 minute.
  3. Add the prawns and halved tomatoes and cook for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add the drained pasta and a splash of cooking water.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley.
