Goulash

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Goulash is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef goulash meat1 kg
  • Onions1 kg
  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Sweet paprika3 tbsp
  • Bay leaves2 leaves
  • White bread1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Finely chop the onion and fry slowly in oil for 15 minutes until golden.
  2. Add the beef and fry for 10 minutes.
  3. Add paprika, tomato paste and bay leaves and cover the meat with water.
  4. Simmer covered for 2 hours. Add water if needed.
  5. Season and serve with bread.
