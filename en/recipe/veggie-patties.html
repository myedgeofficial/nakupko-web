Veggie patties

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Veggie patties is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Courgettes2
  • Carrots2
  • Rolled oats100 g
  • Eggs2
  • Grated cheese100 g
  • Greek yoghurt200 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Grate the courgettes and carrots and squeeze them dry.
  2. Mix with the oats, eggs, cheese and salt.
  3. Shape into patties.
  4. Fry in oil for 4 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with yoghurt.
