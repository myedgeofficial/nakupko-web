Eggs Benedict

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Eggs Benedict is an everyday dish from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs8
  • Toast bread4 slices
  • Ham150 g
  • Butter150 g
  • Lemons1

Method in 4 steps

  1. For the sauce: whisk the yolks and lemon juice over steam, slowly adding melted butter.
  2. Toast the bread and warm the ham.
  3. Poach the eggs in simmering water with vinegar for 3 minutes.
  4. Put ham and an egg on each toast and spoon over the sauce.
