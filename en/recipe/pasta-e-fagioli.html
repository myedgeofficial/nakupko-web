Pasta e fagioli

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pasta e fagioli is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pasta shells250 g
  • Canned beans800 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Carrots1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Parmesan50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion, carrot and garlic in oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes, beans, rosemary and 1 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend half.
  4. Add the pasta and cook until tender.
  5. Serve with parmesan and olive oil.
