Smoothie

  • ⏱ 5 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Smoothie is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 5 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Bananas3
  • Frozen strawberries400 g
  • Plain yoghurt500 g
  • Rolled oats60 g
  • Honey2 tbsp

Method in 3 steps

  1. Put everything in a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth, about 1 minute.
  3. Pour into glasses.
