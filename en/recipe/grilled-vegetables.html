Grilled vegetables

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled vegetables is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Courgettes2
  • Aubergines1
  • Bell peppers2
  • Mushrooms300 g
  • Olive oil5 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Slice the vegetables.
  2. Brush with oil and salt.
  3. Grill for 3–4 minutes on each side.
  4. Mix oil, crushed garlic and parsley.
  5. Drizzle over the vegetables.
