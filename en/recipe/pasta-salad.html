Pasta salad

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pasta salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Fusilli400 g
  • Ham200 g
  • Canned sweetcorn1 can
  • Pickled gherkins1 jar
  • Mayonnaise150 g
  • Edam cheese150 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook, drain and cool the pasta.
  2. Dice the ham, cheese and gherkins.
  3. Mix with the pasta and sweetcorn.
  4. Stir in the mayonnaise and season. Chill for 30 minutes.
