Leek and potato soup

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Leek and potato soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Leek2
  • Potatoes4
  • Butter30 g
  • Milk200 ml
  • Stock cube1
  • Chives1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Slice the leeks and soften them in butter for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the diced potatoes, 1 l of water and the stock cube.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Pour in the milk and blend.
  5. Sprinkle with chives.
