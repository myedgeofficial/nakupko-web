Bacon and eggs

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bacon and eggs is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs8
  • Bacon200 g
  • White bread8 slices
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Fry the bacon until crisp.
  2. Fry the eggs in the same pan.
  3. Halve the tomatoes and fry them briefly.
  4. Serve with bread.
