Chocolate biscuit balls

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate biscuit balls is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Petit beurre biscuits300 g
  • Butter150 g
  • Cocoa powder3 tbsp
  • Icing sugar100 g
  • Milk100 ml

Method in 4 steps

  1. Grind the biscuits.
  2. Add soft butter, cocoa, icing sugar and a little milk.
  3. Mix into a smooth paste.
  4. Roll into balls and chill for 1 hour.
