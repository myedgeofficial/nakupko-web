Đuveč with rice

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Đuveč with rice is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Rice250 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Courgettes1
  • Aubergines1
  • Tomatoes4
  • Onions2
  • Sunflower oil4 tbsp
  • Sweet paprika1 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dice the vegetables.
  2. Fry the onions in oil, add the peppers, aubergine and courgette.
  3. Stir in the rice and paprika, add the tomatoes and 500 ml of water.
  4. Transfer to a baking dish and bake at 180 °C for 35 minutes.
  5. Season and serve.
