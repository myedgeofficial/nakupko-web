Cauliflower cheese

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cauliflower cheese is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cauliflower1
  • Cooking cream200 ml
  • Grated cheese150 g
  • Breadcrumbs3 tbsp
  • Butter30 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Break the cauliflower into florets and boil for 8 minutes.
  3. Put it in a dish and pour over the cream.
  4. Sprinkle with cheese and crumbs and dot with butter.
  5. Bake for 25 minutes.
