Lentil salad

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lentil salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lentils250 g
  • Red onions1
  • Bell peppers1
  • Feta100 g
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Wine vinegar2 tbsp
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the lentils in salted water for 20 minutes until tender but whole.
  2. Drain and cool.
  3. Add the sliced onion, pepper and parsley.
  4. Dress with oil, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  5. Sprinkle with feta.
