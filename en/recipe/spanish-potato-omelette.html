Spanish potato omelette

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spanish potato omelette is an everyday dish from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs6
  • Potatoes600 g
  • Onions1
  • Olive oil100 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Thinly slice the potatoes and onion.
  2. Cook them slowly in oil for 20 minutes until soft.
  3. Drain and mix with the beaten eggs and salt.
  4. Pour into a pan and cook on low heat for 8 minutes.
  5. Flip with a plate and cook for 4 more minutes.
