Grilled cheese and veg

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled cheese and veg is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Grilling cheese500 g
  • Courgettes2
  • Aubergines1
  • Bell peppers2
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Slice the vegetables and brush them with oil.
  2. Grill them for 3–4 minutes per side.
  3. Grill the cheese for 2–3 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Serve together and season.
