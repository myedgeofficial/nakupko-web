Mac and cheese

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Mac and cheese is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Macaroni400 g
  • Milk500 ml
  • Butter40 g
  • White flour40 g
  • Cheddar cheese200 g
  • Grated cheese100 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook and drain the macaroni.
  2. Melt the butter, add the flour and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the milk while stirring and cook until thick.
  4. Stir in both cheeses until melted.
  5. Add the macaroni and mix.
