Oven chicken wings

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Oven chicken wings is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken wings1.2 kg
  • Honey3 tbsp
  • Soy sauce3 tbsp
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Mix the honey, soy sauce, garlic and paprika.
  3. Toss the wings in the sauce and spread them on a tray.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes, turning halfway.
