Red lentil coconut soup

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Red lentil coconut soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Red lentils250 g
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Onions1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Curry powder2 tsp
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion, garlic and curry powder in oil.
  2. Add the lentils, tomatoes and 800 ml of water.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils break down.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk and blend if you like.
  5. Season with salt and lemon juice.
