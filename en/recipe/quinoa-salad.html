Quinoa salad

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Quinoa salad is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Quinoa250 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Avocado2
  • Lemons1
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Rinse the quinoa and cook as on the packet (15 minutes), then cool.
  2. Dice the cucumber and avocado and halve the tomatoes.
  3. Mix everything with the quinoa.
  4. Dress with lemon juice and olive oil and season.
