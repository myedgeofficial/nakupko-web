Egg-fried noodles

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Egg-fried noodles is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chinese noodles300 g
  • Eggs3
  • Frozen stir-fry vegetables400 g
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Spring onions1 bunch
  • Sesame oil1 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook and drain the noodles.
  2. Stir-fry the garlic and vegetables in a wok for 5 minutes.
  3. Push the vegetables aside and scramble the eggs.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce and sesame oil and toss.
  5. Sprinkle with spring onions.
