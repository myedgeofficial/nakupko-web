Plum dumplings

  • ⏱ 1 h 10 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Plum dumplings is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 1 h 10 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • White flour300 g
  • Eggs1
  • Plums16
  • Breadcrumbs100 g
  • Butter80 g
  • Sugarto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes and let them cool.
  2. Add the flour and egg and knead a dough.
  3. Divide it and wrap each piece around a plum.
  4. Simmer the dumplings in salted water for 10 minutes.
  5. Roll in crumbs toasted in butter and sprinkle with sugar.
