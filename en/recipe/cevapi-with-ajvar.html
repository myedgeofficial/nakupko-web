Ćevapi with ajvar

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Ćevapi with ajvar is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Ćevapčići20
  • Lepinja flatbread4
  • Ajvar1 jar
  • Onions2
  • Barbecue charcoal1 bag
  • Beer4

Method in 4 steps

  1. Light the grill and wait until the coals are white.
  2. Grill the ćevapčići for 8–10 minutes, turning.
  3. Warm the flatbreads briefly on the grill.
  4. Serve with chopped onion and ajvar.
