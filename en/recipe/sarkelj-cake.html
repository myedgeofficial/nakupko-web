Šarkelj cake

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Šarkelj cake is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour500 g
  • Fresh yeast1
  • Milk250 ml
  • Butter150 g
  • Eggs4
  • Raisins100 g
  • Sugar100 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the yeast in warm milk, add the flour, butter, eggs and sugar and knead.
  2. Mix in the raisins and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  3. Put the dough in a greased bundt tin and let it rise for 30 more minutes.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes at 180 °C.
  5. Dust with icing sugar.
