Pasta puttanesca

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pasta puttanesca is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Olives100 g
  • Capers2 tbsp
  • Anchovies50 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Chilli peppers1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta.
  2. Fry the garlic, anchovies and chilli in oil.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the olives and capers.
  5. Toss with the pasta and sprinkle with parsley.
