Oven potato wedges

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Oven potato wedges is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1.2 kg
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Sweet paprika1 tsp
  • Rosemaryto taste
  • Mayonnaiseto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C.
  2. Cut the potatoes into wedges.
  3. Toss with oil, paprika, rosemary and salt.
  4. Spread on a tray and roast for 35–40 minutes.
  5. Serve with mayonnaise.
