Beef soup

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Beef soup is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef for soup800 g
  • Carrots3
  • Celeriac1
  • Parsley root1
  • Leek1
  • Soup pasta stars150 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Put the beef in a pot with 3 l of cold water and slowly bring to the boil.
  2. Skim off the foam, add the cleaned vegetables and salt.
  3. Simmer very gently for 2½ hours.
  4. Strain the soup.
  5. Cook the pasta stars in the soup. Serve the meat separately.
