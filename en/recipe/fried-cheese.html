Fried cheese

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fried cheese is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Edam cheese600 g
  • Eggs2
  • White flour80 g
  • Breadcrumbs150 g
  • Frying oil500 ml
  • Tartar sauceto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cut the cheese into thick slices.
  2. Coat in flour, egg and crumbs, then egg and crumbs again.
  3. Fry in hot oil for 1–2 minutes per side.
  4. Serve with tartare sauce.
