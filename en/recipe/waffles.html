Waffles

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Waffles is an everyday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour250 g
  • Milk400 ml
  • Eggs2
  • Butter80 g
  • Baking powder1 bag
  • Sugar2 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder and sugar.
  2. Add the milk, eggs and melted butter.
  3. Heat and grease the waffle iron.
  4. Cook each waffle for 3–4 minutes.
