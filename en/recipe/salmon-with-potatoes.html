Salmon with potatoes

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salmon with potatoes is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Salmon fillet600 g
  • New potatoes800 g
  • Lemons1
  • Dill1 bunch
  • Butter40 g
  • Green beans400 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water (15–20 minutes).
  2. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  3. Put the salmon on a tray, season, add lemon slices and dill.
  4. Bake for 12–15 minutes.
  5. Boil the green beans for 6 minutes and toss with butter.
