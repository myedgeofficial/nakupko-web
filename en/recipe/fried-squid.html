Fried squid

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fried squid is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Squid800 g
  • White flour150 g
  • Frying oil700 ml
  • Lemons2
  • Tartar sauceto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Clean the squid, cut into rings and pat dry.
  2. Season and toss in flour.
  3. Fry in hot oil for 2 minutes until golden.
  4. Serve with lemon and tartare sauce.
