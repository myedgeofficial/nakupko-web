Red lentil dal

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Red lentil dal is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lentils300 g
  • Coconut milk400 ml
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Onions1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Curry powder2 tbsp
  • Basmati rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the chopped onion and garlic in oil for 5 minutes and add the curry powder.
  2. Add the rinsed lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and 500 ml of water.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils are soft.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the rice.
  5. Season and serve with rice.
