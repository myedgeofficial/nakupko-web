Moussaka

  • ⏱ 1 h 50 min
  • Challenging
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Moussaka is a weekend or holiday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 1 h 50 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Aubergines2
  • Potatoes600 g
  • Minced meat500 g
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Milk500 ml
  • Eggs2
  • Parmesan60 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Slice the aubergines and potatoes, season and fry them quickly.
  2. Brown the mince, add the tomatoes and simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  4. Layer potatoes, aubergines and meat in a dish.
  5. Whisk the milk with the eggs and parmesan, pour over and bake for 45 minutes.
