Spinach and ricotta cannelloni

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Spinach and ricotta cannelloni is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lasagne sheets250 g
  • Spinach400 g
  • Cottage cheese (quark)400 g
  • Eggs1
  • Passata500 ml
  • Mozzarella125 g
  • Parmesan50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Blanch, squeeze and chop the spinach and mix with the ricotta, egg and salt.
  2. Boil the lasagne sheets for 1 minute, fill with the mixture and roll up.
  3. Pour the passata into a dish and lay the rolls on top.
  4. Top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  5. Bake at 190 °C for 30 minutes.
