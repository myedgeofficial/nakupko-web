Plum tart

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Plum tart is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour300 g
  • Butter150 g
  • Sugar120 g
  • Eggs2
  • Plums1 kg
  • Cinnamon1 tsp
  • Baking powder1 bag

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar and add the eggs, flour and baking powder.
  3. Spread the batter in a tin.
  4. Halve the plums, press them close together into the batter and sprinkle with cinnamon.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes.
