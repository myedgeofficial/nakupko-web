Shakshuka

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Shakshuka is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs8
  • Peeled tomatoes800 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Onions1
  • Caraway1 tsp
  • Feta150 g
  • White bread1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the sliced onion and pepper in oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the cumin and tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Make little wells in the sauce and crack in the eggs.
  4. Cover and cook for 6–8 minutes until the whites set.
  5. Crumble over the feta and serve with bread.
