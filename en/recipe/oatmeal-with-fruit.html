Oatmeal with fruit

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Oatmeal with fruit is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Rolled oats240 g
  • Milk800 ml
  • Bananas2
  • Blueberries250 g
  • Honey4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Cook the oats in the milk for 5 minutes, stirring.
  2. Slice the banana.
  3. Spoon the porridge into bowls and add banana and blueberries.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
