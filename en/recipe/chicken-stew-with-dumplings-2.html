Chicken stew with dumplings

  • ⏱ 1 h 20 min
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken stew with dumplings is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 1 h 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken thighs800 g
  • Carrots2
  • Potatoes3
  • Onions1
  • White flour3 tbsp
  • Semolina150 g
  • Eggs1
  • Marjoram1 tsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the onion in oil, add the chicken and brown it.
  2. Dust with flour, add 2 l of water, the carrots and marjoram. Simmer for 40 minutes.
  3. Add the diced potatoes.
  4. Mix semolina, egg, a pinch of salt and a little water into a dumpling batter.
  5. Drop spoonfuls into the stew and cook for 10 minutes.
