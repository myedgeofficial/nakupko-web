Sunday brunch

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sunday brunch is an everyday dish from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Croissant4
  • Prosciutto120 g
  • Tolminc cheese150 g
  • Eggs6
  • Orange juice1 l
  • Espresso coffeeto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Make the coffee and pour the juice.
  2. Warm the croissants in the oven for 5 minutes.
  3. Fry or scramble the eggs.
  4. Arrange the prosciutto and cheese on a plate and serve.
