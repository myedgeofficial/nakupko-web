Grilled sea bass

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled sea bass is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sea bass4
  • Lemons2
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Swiss chard1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Light the grill.
  2. Season the fish, brush with oil and fill with lemon and parsley.
  3. Grill for 8–10 minutes per side.
  4. Boil the chard for 5 minutes and dress with garlic and oil.
  5. Serve together.
