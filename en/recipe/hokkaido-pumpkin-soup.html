Hokkaido pumpkin soup

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Hokkaido pumpkin soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Hokkaido pumpkin1
  • Onions1
  • Ginger1
  • Butter20 g
  • Cream100 ml
  • Pumpkin seed oil2 tbsp
  • Pumpkin seeds40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Wash and halve the pumpkin, remove the seeds and chop (no need to peel).
  2. Fry the onion and ginger in butter, add the pumpkin and 1 l of water.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes and blend with the cream.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve with pumpkin seed oil and seeds.
