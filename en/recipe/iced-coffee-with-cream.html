Iced coffee with cream

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Iced coffee with cream is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Espresso coffee4
  • Milk400 ml
  • Cream200 ml
  • Sugar4 tsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Make the espresso and let it cool.
  2. Put ice and milk in glasses.
  3. Pour over the coffee and sweeten to taste.
  4. Top with whipped cream.
