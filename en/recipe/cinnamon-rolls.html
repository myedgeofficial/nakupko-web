Cinnamon rolls

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Medium
  • 10 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cinnamon rolls is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 10 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour500 g
  • Fresh yeast1
  • Milk250 ml
  • Butter120 g
  • Eggs1
  • Sugar80 g
  • Brown sugar100 g
  • Cinnamon2 tbsp
  • Cheese spread150 g
  • Icing sugar80 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the yeast in warm milk, add the flour, half the butter, the egg and sugar and knead. Rise for 1 hour.
  2. Roll out, spread with soft butter and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Roll up, slice into rolls and pack them in a tin. Rise for 30 minutes.
  4. Bake for 25 minutes at 180 °C.
  5. Mix the cream cheese with icing sugar and spread over the warm rolls.
