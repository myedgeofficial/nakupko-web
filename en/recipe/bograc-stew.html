Bograč stew

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bograč stew is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef goulash meat500 g
  • Pork shoulder500 g
  • Potatoes800 g
  • Onions600 g
  • Bell peppers2
  • Sweet paprika3 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut the meat into cubes.
  2. Fry lots of onion in oil for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the meat, paprika and water and simmer for 2 hours.
  4. Add sliced peppers and diced potatoes.
  5. Cook for another 30 minutes and season.
