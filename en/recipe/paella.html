Paella

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Medium
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Paella is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Risotto rice350 g
  • Chicken thighs500 g
  • Prawns300 g
  • Bell peppers1
  • Frozen peas150 g
  • Saffron1 bag
  • Chorizo100 g
  • Stock cube1
  • Lemons1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Brown the chicken and chorizo in a large pan.
  2. Add the pepper and rice and stir.
  3. Pour in 1 l of stock with the saffron and stop stirring. Cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the prawns and peas and cook for 8 more minutes.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with lemon.
