Mussels buzara

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Mussels buzara is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Mussels1.5 kg
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • White wine200 ml
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Breadcrumbs3 tbsp
  • White bread1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Wash the mussels and throw away any open ones.
  2. Fry the garlic in oil for 1 minute.
  3. Add the mussels and wine, cover and cook for 5 minutes until they open.
  4. Sprinkle with crumbs and parsley and stir.
  5. Serve with bread.
