Pumpkin risotto

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pumpkin risotto is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Risotto rice320 g
  • Butternut squash800 g
  • Onions1
  • Stock cube1
  • Butter40 g
  • Parmesan60 g
  • White wine150 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Peel and dice the squash.
  2. Fry the onion in butter for 3 minutes, then add the squash and rice.
  3. Pour in the wine, then the hot stock bit by bit.
  4. Stir for about 20 minutes until creamy.
  5. Stir in butter and parmesan.
