Panna cotta

  • ⏱ 4 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Panna cotta is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 4 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Cream500 ml
  • Milk200 ml
  • Sugar80 g
  • Gelatine1 bag
  • Vanilla sugar1 bag
  • Frozen fruit300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Soak the gelatine in cold water for 5 minutes.
  2. Heat the cream, milk, sugar and vanilla sugar without boiling.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the squeezed gelatine.
  4. Pour into glasses and chill for at least 4 hours.
  5. Warm the fruit with a spoon of sugar and pour over.
