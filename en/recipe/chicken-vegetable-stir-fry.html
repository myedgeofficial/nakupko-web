Chicken vegetable stir-fry

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken vegetable stir-fry is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Frozen stir-fry vegetables750 g
  • Soy sauce5 tbsp
  • Ginger1
  • Basmati rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Cut the chicken into strips and stir-fry in hot oil for 5 minutes.
  3. Add grated ginger and the frozen veg and fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce and toss.
  5. Serve with rice.
