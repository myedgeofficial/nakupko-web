Mushroom stroganoff

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Mushroom stroganoff is an everyday dish from our “Meat-free” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Mushrooms600 g
  • Onions1
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Sweet paprika1 tsp
  • Rice300 g
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Fry the chopped onion in oil for 4 minutes.
  3. Add the sliced mushrooms and fry for 8 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with paprika, stir in the sour cream and heat through.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with rice.
