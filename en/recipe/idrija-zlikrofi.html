Idrija žlikrofi

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Idrija žlikrofi is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour400 g
  • Eggs2
  • Potatoes800 g
  • Smoked ham150 g
  • Onions1
  • Chives1 bunch
  • Marjoramto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 5 steps

  1. Knead the flour, eggs and a little water into a dough and let it rest.
  2. Boil and mash the potatoes and add fried onion, ham, chives and marjoram.
  3. Roll the dough thin and cut out circles.
  4. Put a little filling on each and pinch closed into a hat shape.
  5. Cook in salted water for 5 minutes.
