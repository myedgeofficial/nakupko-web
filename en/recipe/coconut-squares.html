Coconut squares

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Coconut squares is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour300 g
  • Eggs4
  • Sugar200 g
  • Milk200 ml
  • Baking powder1 bag
  • Cooking chocolate200 g
  • Desiccated coconut150 g
  • Butter50 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Beat the eggs with the sugar, add the milk, flour and baking powder.
  2. Bake in a tin at 180 °C for 25 minutes and cool.
  3. Cut the sponge into squares.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the butter and a little milk.
  5. Dip the squares in chocolate and roll in coconut.
