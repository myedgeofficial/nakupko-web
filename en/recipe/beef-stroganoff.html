Beef stroganoff

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Beef stroganoff is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Beef steak600 g
  • Mushrooms300 g
  • Onions1
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Mustard1 tbsp
  • Rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Cut the beef into strips, sear quickly and set aside.
  3. In the same pan fry the onion and mushrooms for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the sour cream and mustard, return the beef and heat through.
  5. Serve with rice.
