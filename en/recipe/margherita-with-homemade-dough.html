Margherita with homemade dough

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Margherita with homemade dough is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pizza flour (00)500 g
  • Dry yeast1 bag
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Mozzarella250 g
  • Basil1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Knead the flour, yeast, salt, oil and 300 ml of lukewarm water into a dough.
  2. Cover and leave to rise for 1½ hours.
  3. Heat the oven as high as it goes.
  4. Stretch the dough, spread with crushed tomatoes and add mozzarella.
  5. Bake for 8–10 minutes and add basil.
