Fruit salad

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fruit salad is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Apples2
  • Bananas2
  • Oranges2
  • Grapes300 g
  • Kiwi3
  • Honey2 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Peel and chop the fruit.
  2. Halve the grapes.
  3. Mix everything in a bowl.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
