Courgette lemon pasta

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Courgette lemon pasta is a quick dish for busy days from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Courgettes3
  • Lemons1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Parmesan60 g
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
  2. Grate the courgettes or cut them into thin ribbons.
  3. Fry the garlic and courgettes in oil for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the spaghetti, lemon zest and juice and a splash of cooking water.
  5. Sprinkle with parmesan.
