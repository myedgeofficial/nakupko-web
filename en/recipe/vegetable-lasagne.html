Vegetable lasagne

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Medium
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vegetable lasagne is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lasagne sheets250 g
  • Courgettes2
  • Aubergines1
  • Bell peppers2
  • Passata700 ml
  • Milk500 ml
  • Butter50 g
  • White flour50 g
  • Mozzarella250 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dice the vegetables, fry in oil for 10 minutes and add the sauce.
  2. Make the white sauce: butter, flour, then milk while stirring.
  3. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  4. Layer sheets, vegetables and white sauce in a dish, mozzarella on top.
  5. Bake for 40 minutes.
