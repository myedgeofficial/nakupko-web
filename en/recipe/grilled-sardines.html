Grilled sardines

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Grilled sardines is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Fresh sardines1 kg
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Lemons1
  • Swiss chard500 g
  • Potatoes600 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Clean the fish, salt it and brush with oil.
  2. Grill on a hot grill or pan for 3–4 minutes on each side.
  3. Mix oil, chopped garlic and parsley and pour over the fish.
  4. Boil the potatoes and chard and dress with oil.
  5. Serve with lemon.
