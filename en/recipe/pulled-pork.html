Pulled pork

  • ⏱ 5 h
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pulled pork is a weekend or holiday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 5 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork shoulder1.5 kg
  • BBQ sauce300 ml
  • Brown sugar2 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika2 tsp
  • Burger buns6
  • Cabbage1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Rub the pork with salt, brown sugar and smoked paprika.
  2. Roast covered for 4½ hours at 150 °C.
  3. Pull the meat apart with forks and mix with BBQ sauce.
  4. Finely shred the cabbage and salt it.
  5. Serve in buns with the slaw.
