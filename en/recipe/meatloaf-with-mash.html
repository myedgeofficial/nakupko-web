Meatloaf with mash

  • ⏱ 1 h 20 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Meatloaf with mash is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 1 h 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Minced meat800 g
  • Eggs2
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Onions1
  • Bacon100 g
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Milk200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Mix the mince with egg, crumbs, chopped onion and salt.
  3. Shape a loaf, wrap it in bacon and bake for 1 hour.
  4. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes and mash with milk.
  5. Slice and serve with the mash.
