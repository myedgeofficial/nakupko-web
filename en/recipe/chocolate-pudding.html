Chocolate pudding

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chocolate pudding is a quick dish for busy days from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Milk1 l
  • Pudding powder2 bags
  • Sugar80 g
  • Cream200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the pudding powder with sugar and a little cold milk.
  2. Bring the rest of the milk to the boil.
  3. Stir in the pudding and cook for 1 minute, stirring.
  4. Pour into cups and let it cool.
  5. Serve with whipped cream.
