Potato salad with pumpkin seed oil

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Potato salad with pumpkin seed oil is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Red onions1
  • Pumpkin seed oil4 tbsp
  • Apple cider vinegar3 tbsp
  • Stock cube1
  • Chives1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel and slice.
  2. Dissolve the stock cube in 150 ml of hot water and pour over the potatoes.
  3. Add the finely chopped onion and vinegar.
  4. Dress with pumpkin seed oil, salt and pepper.
  5. Sprinkle with chives.
