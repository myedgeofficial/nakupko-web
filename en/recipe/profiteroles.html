Profiteroles

  • ⏱ 1 h 30 min
  • Challenging
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Profiteroles is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 1 h 30 min, the difficulty is challenging and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour150 g
  • Butter100 g
  • Eggs4
  • Cream500 ml
  • Cooking chocolate150 g
  • Icing sugar40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Bring the butter to the boil with 250 ml of water, tip in the flour and stir until it leaves the pan.
  3. Cool and beat in the eggs one at a time.
  4. Pipe small balls onto a tray and bake for 25 minutes. Don't open the oven while baking.
  5. Fill with whipped cream and pour over melted chocolate.
