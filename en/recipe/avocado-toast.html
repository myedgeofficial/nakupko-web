Avocado toast

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Avocado toast is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Seeded bread8 slices
  • Avocado3
  • Eggs4
  • Lemons1
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Toast the bread.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice and salt.
  3. Fry or soft-boil the eggs.
  4. Spread the avocado on the toast and top with egg and halved tomatoes.
