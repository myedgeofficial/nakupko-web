Penne arrabbiata

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Penne arrabbiata is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Penne400 g
  • Peeled tomatoes800 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Chilli peppers1
  • Olive oil4 tbsp
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted water.
  2. Fry the garlic and chilli in oil for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, season and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the drained pasta and toss.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley.
