Prawns alla buzara

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Prawns alla buzara is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Prawns600 g
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • White wine150 ml
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Breadcrumbs2 tbsp
  • Parsley1 bunch
  • White bread8 slices

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fry the garlic in olive oil.
  2. Add the tomatoes and wine and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the prawns and cook for 4 minutes.
  4. Stir in the breadcrumbs to thicken and add the parsley.
  5. Serve with bread for dipping.
