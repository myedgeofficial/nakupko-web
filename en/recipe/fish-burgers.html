Fish burgers

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Fish burgers is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Hake fillet600 g
  • Burger buns4
  • Breadcrumbs80 g
  • Eggs1
  • Iceberg lettuce1
  • Tartar sauceto taste

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Finely chop the fish and mix with egg, crumbs and salt.
  2. Shape 4 patties.
  3. Fry them in oil for 4 minutes per side.
  4. Toast the buns and build the burgers with lettuce and tartare sauce.
