Tarragon potica

  • ⏱ 3 h
  • Challenging
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tarragon potica is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Desserts & baking” collection. It takes about 3 h, the difficulty is challenging and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • White flour600 g
  • Fresh yeast1
  • Milk300 ml
  • Butter150 g
  • Eggs4
  • Sugar150 g
  • Cottage cheese (quark)300 g
  • Sour cream200 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the yeast in warm milk, add the flour, butter, eggs and sugar and knead.
  2. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
  3. Mix the quark with sour cream, egg and sugar. Add tarragon to taste.
  4. Roll out, spread with filling, roll up and put in a tin. Rise for 30 more minutes.
  5. Bake for 1 hour at 170 °C.
