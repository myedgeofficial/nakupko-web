Chicken in gorgonzola sauce

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken in gorgonzola sauce is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Gorgonzola120 g
  • Cream200 ml
  • Butter20 g
  • Tagliatelle400 g
  • Walnuts40 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the pasta as on the packet.
  2. Cut the chicken into strips and fry in butter for 6 minutes.
  3. Add the cream and gorgonzola and stir until the cheese melts.
  4. Toss in the drained pasta.
  5. Sprinkle with chopped walnuts.
