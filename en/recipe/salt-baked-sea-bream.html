Salt-baked sea bream

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salt-baked sea bream is an everyday dish from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sea bream2
  • Sea salt2 kg
  • Lemons1
  • Rosemary1 bunch
  • Swiss chard500 g
  • Potatoes600 g
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Fill the cleaned fish with lemon and rosemary.
  2. Put a layer of salt in a tin, add the fish and cover it with slightly moistened salt.
  3. Bake at 200 °C for 30 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile boil the potatoes and chard and dress with oil.
  5. Crack the salt crust and serve the fish.
