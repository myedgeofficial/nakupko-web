Roast pork neck

  • ⏱ 2 h
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Roast pork neck is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 2 h, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pork neck1.2 kg
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Caraway1 tsp
  • Potatoes1 kg
  • Onions2
  • Beer330 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Rub the meat with salt, pepper, crushed garlic and caraway.
  2. Put it in a roasting tin with the onions and beer and cover with foil.
  3. Roast at 180 °C for 1 hour.
  4. Uncover, add potato wedges and roast for 50 more minutes, basting now and then.
  5. Let the meat rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
