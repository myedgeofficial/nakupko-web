Honey mustard chicken thighs

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Honey mustard chicken thighs is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken thighs1 kg
  • Honey3 tbsp
  • Mustard2 tbsp
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • New potatoes800 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the honey, mustard, crushed garlic, oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the thighs with the sauce and put them in a tin with the potatoes.
  3. Roast at 200 °C for 40 minutes.
  4. Turn the meat halfway and baste with the juices.
  5. Serve with a salad.
