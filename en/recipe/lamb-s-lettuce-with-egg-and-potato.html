Lamb's lettuce with egg and potato

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Lamb's lettuce with egg and potato is an everyday dish from our “Salads & light meals” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Lamb's lettuce200 g
  • Eggs4
  • Potatoes4
  • Pumpkin seed oil4 tbsp
  • Vinegar2 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins (20 minutes), peel and slice.
  2. Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, peel and slice.
  3. Wash the lamb's lettuce.
  4. Mix everything with pumpkin seed oil, vinegar and salt.
