Gyros pita

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Gyros pita is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Pita bread4
  • Greek yoghurt300 g
  • Cucumbers1
  • Tomatoes2
  • Red onions1
  • Garlic2 cloves

Method in 4 steps

  1. Slice the chicken and fry with salt and pepper.
  2. Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic and grated cucumber.
  3. Slice the tomato and onion.
  4. Warm the pitta and fill with chicken, veg and sauce.
