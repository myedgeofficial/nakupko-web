Cheese burek with ready dough

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cheese burek with ready dough is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Filo pastry1 pack
  • Cottage cheese (quark)500 g
  • Feta200 g
  • Eggs3
  • Sour cream200 ml
  • Sunflower oil100 ml

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Mix the quark, crumbled feta, eggs and sour cream.
  3. Brush the pastry sheets with oil, spread the filling and roll into logs.
  4. Coil the logs into a spiral in a tin and brush with oil.
  5. Bake for 35 minutes until golden.
