Chicken ramen

  • ⏱ 1 h
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken ramen is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 1 h, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast400 g
  • Instant noodles4
  • Eggs4
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Ginger30 g
  • Spring onions1 bunch
  • Stock cube2

Method in 5 steps

  1. Dissolve the stock cube in 1.5 l of water, add ginger and soy sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  2. Poach the chicken in the broth for 15 minutes, lift out and slice.
  3. Boil the eggs for 7 minutes and peel.
  4. Cook the noodles in the broth.
  5. Put noodles, broth, chicken, half an egg and spring onion in each bowl.
