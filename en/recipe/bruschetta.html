Bruschetta

  • ⏱ 15 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Bruschetta is a quick dish for busy days from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 15 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Ciabatta1
  • Tomatoes4
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Basil1 bunch
  • Olive oil4 tbsp

Method in 4 steps

  1. Slice and toast the bread.
  2. Rub it with garlic.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and mix with basil, oil and salt.
  4. Spoon onto the bread and serve straight away.
