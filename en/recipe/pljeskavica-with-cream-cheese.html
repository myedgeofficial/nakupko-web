Pljeskavica with cream cheese

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pljeskavica with cream cheese is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Burger patties4
  • Lepinja flatbread4
  • Cream cheese150 g
  • Red onions1
  • Ajvar1 jar
  • Tomatoes2

Method in 5 steps

  1. Grill the patties for 5 minutes on each side.
  2. Toast the flatbreads briefly.
  3. Spread the flatbreads with cream cheese.
  4. Add the patty, onion and tomato.
  5. Serve with ajvar.
