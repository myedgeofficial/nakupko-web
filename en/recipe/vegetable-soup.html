Vegetable soup

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Vegetable soup is an everyday dish from our “Soups & stews” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Carrots2
  • Potatoes2
  • Courgettes1
  • Leek1
  • Celeriac0.5
  • Frozen peas150 g
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Stock cube1
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cut all the vegetables into small cubes.
  2. Fry the leek, carrot and celeriac in oil for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the potato, 1.5 l of water and the stock cube. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the courgette and peas and cook for 10 more minutes.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and parsley.
