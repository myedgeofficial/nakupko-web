Salmon poke bowl

  • ⏱ 30 min
  • Easy
  • 8 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Salmon poke bowl is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 8 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Salmon fillet400 g
  • Sushi rice300 g
  • Avocado1
  • Cucumbers1
  • Carrots1
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Sesame seeds2 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice and mix it with the rice vinegar.
  2. Dice the salmon and marinate it in soy sauce for 10 minutes.
  3. Slice the vegetables.
  4. Put rice in bowls and top with the salmon and vegetables.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
