Pad thai

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Medium
  • 7 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Pad thai is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 7 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Rice noodles300 g
  • Prawns300 g
  • Eggs3
  • Peanuts80 g
  • Limes2
  • Soy sauce4 tbsp
  • Spring onions1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Soak the noodles in hot water as on the packet.
  2. Fry the prawns in oil for 2 minutes and push to the side.
  3. Crack in the eggs and scramble.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce and lime juice and toss.
  5. Sprinkle with peanuts and spring onion.
