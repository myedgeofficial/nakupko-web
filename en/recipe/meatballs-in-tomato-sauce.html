Meatballs in tomato sauce

  • ⏱ 50 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Meatballs in tomato sauce is a dish for when you have a bit more time from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 50 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Minced meat600 g
  • Eggs1
  • Breadcrumbs60 g
  • Passata700 ml
  • Onions1
  • Spaghetti400 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Mix the mince with egg, crumbs and salt and roll into balls.
  2. Brown the meatballs.
  3. Add the onion and sauce and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti.
  5. Serve together.
