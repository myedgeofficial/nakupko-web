Jota

  • ⏱ 1 h 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Jota is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 1 h 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Sauerkraut500 g
  • Canned beans2 cans
  • Potatoes3
  • Pork ribs500 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Method in 4 steps

  1. Simmer the ribs in water for 45 minutes.
  2. Add the sauerkraut, bay leaves and diced potato and cook for 30 minutes.
  3. Add the drained beans and crushed garlic.
  4. Cook for another 15 minutes and season.
