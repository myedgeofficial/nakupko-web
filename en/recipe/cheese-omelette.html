Cheese omelette

  • ⏱ 10 min
  • Easy
  • 4 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Cheese omelette is a quick dish for busy days from our “Breakfast” collection. It takes about 10 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 4 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Eggs8
  • Grated cheese100 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Chives1 bunch

Method in 4 steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with salt.
  2. Pour into a buttered pan and cook over medium heat.
  3. When the bottom has set, sprinkle with cheese and chives.
  4. Fold and serve.
