Barley stew

  • ⏱ 2 h 30 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Barley stew is a weekend or holiday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 2 h 30 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Pearl barley200 g
  • Dried beans200 g
  • Smoked ham400 g
  • Carrots2
  • Potatoes2
  • Bay leaves2 leaves

Method in 5 steps

  1. Soak the beans overnight (if not tinned).
  2. Put the barley, beans and ham in a pot with 3 l of water and simmer for 1 hour.
  3. Add diced carrot, potato and bay leaves.
  4. Simmer for another 45 minutes.
  5. Cut the ham back into the pot and season.
