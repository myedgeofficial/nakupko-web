Sesame chicken

  • ⏱ 25 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Sesame chicken is an everyday dish from our “Meat” collection. It takes about 25 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Sesame seeds60 g
  • Eggs2
  • White flour50 g
  • Rice300 g
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp

Method in 5 steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Cut the chicken into escalopes and season.
  3. Coat in flour, egg and sesame seeds.
  4. Fry in oil for 4 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with rice and soy sauce.
