Burgers and sausages

  • ⏱ 35 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Burgers and sausages is an everyday dish from our “BBQ & party” collection. It takes about 35 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Burger patties4
  • Grill sausages8
  • Lepinja flatbread4
  • Ajvar1 jar
  • Mustardto taste
  • Barbecue charcoal1 bag

Items marked “to taste” are seasonings and sauces most people already have at home. If you don't, add them to your list.

Method in 4 steps

  1. Light the grill and wait until the coals are white.
  2. Grill the patties for 5 minutes per side.
  3. Grill the sausages for 10–12 minutes, turning.
  4. Serve with flatbreads, ajvar and mustard.
