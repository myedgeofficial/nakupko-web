Buckwheat with mushrooms

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Buckwheat with mushrooms is an everyday dish from our “Slovenian classics” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Buckwheat groats300 g
  • Mushrooms400 g
  • Onions1
  • Sour cream200 g
  • Parsley1 bunch

Method in 4 steps

  1. Rinse the buckwheat and cook in twice the amount of salted water (20 minutes).
  2. Fry the onion and mushrooms in oil for 8 minutes.
  3. Mix with the buckwheat.
  4. Stir in the sour cream and parsley.
