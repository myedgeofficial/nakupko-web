Tuna steak with salad

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Medium
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Tuna steak with salad is a quick dish for busy days from our “Fish & seafood” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Fresh tuna steak600 g
  • Rocket150 g
  • Cherry tomatoes250 g
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Sesame seeds2 tbsp
  • Limes1

Method in 5 steps

  1. Season the tuna and coat it in sesame.
  2. Sear in a very hot pan for 1 minute per side.
  3. Put the rocket and tomatoes on plates.
  4. Slice the tuna and lay it on the salad.
  5. Dress with soy sauce and lime juice.
