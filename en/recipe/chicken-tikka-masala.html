Chicken tikka masala

  • ⏱ 45 min
  • Medium
  • 9 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Chicken tikka masala is an everyday dish from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 45 min, the difficulty is medium and you need 9 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Chicken breast600 g
  • Plain yoghurt150 g
  • Garam masala2 tbsp
  • Peeled tomatoes400 g
  • Cream150 ml
  • Onions1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Ginger1
  • Basmati rice300 g

Method in 5 steps

  1. Marinate the diced chicken in yoghurt and half the garam masala for 20 minutes.
  2. Sear the meat in a pan and set aside.
  3. Fry the onion, garlic, ginger and remaining spice, add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cream and chicken and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice.
