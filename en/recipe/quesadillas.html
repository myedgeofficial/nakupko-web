Quesadillas

  • ⏱ 20 min
  • Easy
  • 5 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Quesadillas is a quick dish for busy days from our “World flavours” collection. It takes about 20 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 5 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Tortillas8
  • Grated cheese250 g
  • Chicken breast300 g
  • Bell peppers1
  • Sour cream200 g

Method in 4 steps

  1. Slice the chicken and fry it with the pepper.
  2. Sprinkle cheese on half a tortilla, add chicken and fold.
  3. Cook in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Cut into wedges and serve with sour cream.
