Baked feta pasta

  • ⏱ 40 min
  • Easy
  • 6 ingredients
  • Serves 4

Baked feta pasta is an everyday dish from our “Pasta & risotto” collection. It takes about 40 min, the difficulty is easy and you need 6 ingredients.

Ingredients for 4 people

  • Penne400 g
  • Cherry tomatoes500 g
  • Feta200 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Olive oil5 tbsp
  • Basil1 bunch

Method in 5 steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Put the tomatoes, garlic and olive oil in a dish with the feta in the middle.
  3. Bake for 30 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook the pasta.
  5. Mash the feta and tomatoes, add the pasta and basil and mix.
