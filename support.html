The list doesn't open in the store

iPhone: in Settings → Nakupko set Location to »Always«, turn on »Precise Location« and Notifications.
Android: in Settings → Apps → Nakupko → Permissions set Location to »Allow all the time« and turn on Notifications.
In the app, under Settings, turn on »Detect store automatically«.

Shared list

In the app under Settings tap »Create shared list« and send the code to your partner. They enter it under »Join«.

Nakupko Plus subscription

On iPhone manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. Restore a purchase in the app with »Restore purchase«.

Cheapest store

The cheapest store is only a rough estimate. Prices are collected once a day from public sources and may differ from those in the store. The price in the store applies. We have no insight into stock. Opening hours are for information only. Nakupko accepts no liability for differences.
