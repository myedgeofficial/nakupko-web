About

Nakupko is a Slovenian shopping list that opens by itself when you walk into a store. It is published by REMAKO d.o.o. from Rače, Slovenia.

Why Nakupko

We built Nakupko because we were tired of digging for the list in the middle of a store and forgetting things. We wanted a list that is there when you need it, that you can share with your household and that helps with cooking.

What you will find here

  • A web shopping list with no sign-up, sent to your phone with a QR code.
  • 345 recipes with ingredients scaled to the number of people.
  • The Nakupko app for iPhone and Android.

How our content is made

Recipes are prepared by the Nakupko team and checked so the amounts make sense for 4 people and the steps are clear. We do not publish prices. If you spot a mistake, write to us.
